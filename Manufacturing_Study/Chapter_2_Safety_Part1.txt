Is chapter mein hum padhenge factory mein safety aur hygiene (saaf-safai) maintain karne ke rules. Yeh exam ke liye bahut important chapter hai.

【２．１】 基本作業 (Kihon sagyou - Basic Operations)
【２．１．１】 安全衛生の心得 (Anzen eisei no kokoroe - Safety & Hygiene Knowledge/Rules)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[JAPANESE SENTENCE 1]
製造現場には高温、重量物、機械設備、化学物質など、多くの危険があります。
(Seizou genba ni wa kouon, juuryoubutsu, kikai setsubi, kagaku busshitsu nado, ooku no kiken ga arimasu.)

[Word-by-Word]
・製造現場 (seizou genba) = Manufacturing site / Factory floor
・高温 (kouon) = High temperature / Bahut zyada garmi
・重量物 (juuryoubutsu) = Heavy objects / Bhaari saaman
・機械設備 (kikai setsubi) = Machinery and equipment / Machinayein
・化学物質 (kagaku busshitsu) = Chemical substances / Chemicals
・多くの (ooku no) = Bahut saare
・危険 (kiken) = Danger / Khatra

[Overall Meaning]
Factory floor par bahut zyada garmi, bhaari saaman, machines aur chemicals jaise bahut saare khatre hote hain.

---

[JAPANESE SENTENCE 2]
これらの危険から、作業者の命と健康を守るための取組みが、安全衛生活動です。
(Korera no kiken kara, sagyousha no inochi to kenkou wo mamoru tame no torikumi ga, anzen eisei katsudou desu.)

[Word-by-Word]
・これらの (korera no) = In sab
・作業者 (sagyousha) = Workers / Kaam karne wale
・命と健康 (inochi to kenkou) = Jaan aur Sehat
・守るため (mamoru tame) = Bachane/Protect karne ke liye
・取組み (torikumi) = Efforts / Initiatives
・活動 (katsudou) = Activities

[Overall Meaning]
In sab khatron se workers ki jaan aur sehat ko bachane ke liye jo efforts kiye jaate hain, unhe "Safety and Hygiene Activities" (Anzen Eisei Katsudou) kehte hain.

---

[JAPANESE SENTENCE 3]
作業者が仕事や通勤が原因で、負傷したり、病気にかかったりすることを労働災害といいます。
(Sagyousha ga shigoto ya tsuukin ga gen'in de, fushou shitari, byouki ni kakattari suru koto wo roudou saigai to iimasu.)

[Word-by-Word]
・仕事 (shigoto) = Work / Kaam
・通勤 (tsuukin) = Commute / Kaam par aana-jana
・原因 (gen'in) = Cause / Wajah
・負傷したり (fushou shitari) = Injured hona / Chot lagna
・病気にかかったり (byouki ni kakattari) = Bimaar pad jana
・労働災害 (roudou saigai) = Occupational accident / Work-related accident

[Overall Meaning]
Kaam karne ya kaam par aane-jaane ki wajah se agar kisi worker ko chot lag jaye ya woh bimaar pad jaye, toh use "Occupational Accident" (Roudou Saigai) kehte hain.

---

【安全のための重要な心得】(Anzen no tame no juuyou na kokoroe - Safety ke liye important rules)

① 職場のルールを守ってください。作業者を守るためのものです。ルールを守らないと周りの人にも危険が及びます。
(Shokuba no ruuru wo mamotte kudasai. Sagyousha wo mamoru tame no mono desu. Ruuru wo mamoranai to mawari no hito ni mo kiken ga oyobimasu.)
Word-by-word meaning:
・職場 (shokuba) = Workplace / Kaam ki jagah
・守る (mamoru) = Follow karna
・周りの人 (mawari no hito) = Aas-paas ke log
・及ぶ (oyobu) = Puhanchta hai / Spreads
Overall: Workplace ke rules follow karein. Yeh workers ko protect karne ke liye hain. Agar rules follow nahi karenge toh aas-paas ke logon ko bhi khatra hoga.

② 知らない機械や道具には、勝手に触ってはいけません。必ず、上司に聞いてください。
(Shiranai kikai ya dougu ni wa, katte ni sawatte wa ikemasen. Kanarazu, joushi ni kiite kudasai.)
Word-by-word meaning:
・知らない (shiranai) = Unknown / Jo pata na ho
・道具 (dougu) = Tools / Auzaar
・勝手に (katte ni) = Apni marzi se / Without permission
・触る (sawaru) = Touch karna
・必ず (kanarazu) = Zaroor / Without fail
・上司 (joushi) = Boss / Manager
Overall: Jin machines ya tools ke baare mein aap nahi jaante, unhe apni marzi se touch mat karein. Zaroor apne boss se puchein.

③ 道具は丁寧に扱ってください。使った後は、正しい場所に保管してください。投げ渡したり、乱雑においたりしてはいけません。
(Dougu wa teinei ni atsukatte kudasai. Tsukatta ato wa, tadashii basho ni hokan shite kudasai. Nagewatashitari, ranzatsu ni oitari shite wa ikemasen.)
Word-by-word meaning:
・丁寧に (teinei ni) = Carefully / Aaram se
・扱う (atsukau) = Handle karna
・保管する (hokan suru) = Store/Rakhna
・投げ渡す (nagewatasu) = Fenk kar dena
・乱雑に (ranzatsu ni) = Messy way mein / Bikhra kar
Overall: Tools ko carefully handle karein. Use karne ke baad sahi jagah par rakhein. Unhe fenk kar na dein aur na hi bikhra kar rakhein.

④ 危険がなくても、法律やルールを守ってください。日本の法律は、作業者一人ひとりを守るためにあります。
(Kiken ga nakute mo, houritsu ya ruuru wo mamotte kudasai. Nihon no houritsu wa, sagyousha hitorihitori wo mamoru tame ni arimasu.)
Word-by-word meaning:
・法律 (houritsu) = Law / Kanoon
・一人ひとり (hitorihitori) = Har ek insaan ko
Overall: Agar khatra na bhi ho, tab bhi kanoon aur rules ko follow karein. Japan ke kanoon har ek worker ko protect karne ke liye hain.

⑤ 何か異常（変な音、におい、振動など）に気づいたら、機械を止めて上司に報告し、その指示に従ってください。
(Nanika ijou (hen na oto, nioi, shindou nado) ni kizuitara, kikai wo tomete joushi ni houkoku shi, sono shiji ni shitagatte kudasai.)
Word-by-word meaning:
・異常 (ijou) = Abnormality / Kuch ajeeb
・変な音 (hen na oto) = Ajeeb awaaz
・におい (nioi) = Smell / Badbu
・振動 (shindou) = Vibration / Hilna
・気づく (kizuku) = Notice karna
・報告し (houkoku shi) = Report karna
・指示 (shiji) = Instructions
・従う (shitagau) = Follow karna / Maanna
Overall: Agar aapko kuch bhi ajeeb lage (jaise ajeeb awaaz, smell, ya vibration), toh machine rok kar boss ko report karein aur unke instructions follow karein.

【２．１．２】 労働安全衛生法
(Roudou anzen eisei hou - Industrial Safety and Health Act)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[JAPANESE SENTENCE]
労働安全衛生法は、労働者の安全と健康を守るために定められた、日本の法律です。主な内容は、つぎの３つです。
(Roudou anzen eiseihou wa, roudousha no anzen to kenkou wo mamoru tame ni sadamerareta, nihon no houritsu desu. Omo na naiyou wa, tsugi no mittsu desu.)

[Word-by-Word]
・労働安全衛生法 (roudou anzen eisei hou) = Work safety & health law
・定められた (sadamerareta) = Banaya gaya / Decided
・主な内容 (omo na naiyou) = Main contents
・つぎの３つ (tsugi no mittsu) = Neeche diye gaye 3

[Overall Meaning]
"Industrial Safety and Health Act" workers ki safety aur health ko bachane ke liye banaya gaya Japan ka kanoon hai. Iske main 3 points hain:

（１）労働災害防止のための危害防止基準の確立
(Roudou saigai boushi no tame no kigai boushi kijun no kakuritsu)
= Work accidents rokne ke liye hazard prevention standards banana. (Company aur worker dono ko rules follow karne hote hain).

（２）責任体制の明確化
(Sekinin taisei no meikakuka)
= Responsibility system ko clear karna.
(Company mein "Safety and Health Committee" (安全衛生委員会 - Anzen Eisei Iinkai) aur safety managers, company doctors hone chahiye jo safety check karein).

（３）自主的活動の促進
(Jishuteki katsudou no sokushin)
= Voluntary activities ko badhava dena.
(Har worker khud safety ke baare mein soche, khatra pehchane aur precautions le).

【２．１．３】 あいさつ
(Aisatsu - Greetings)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[JAPANESE SENTENCE]
事故を防ぐための取組みの中で、もっとも基本的で大切なことがあいさつです。
(Jiko wo fusegu tame no torikumi no naka de, mottomo kihonteki de taisetsu na koto ga aisatsu desu.)

[Word-by-Word]
・事故 (jiko) = Accident
・防ぐ (fusegu) = Rokna / Prevent
・取組み (torikumi) = Efforts
・もっとも (mottomo) = Sabse zyada
・基本的 (kihonteki) = Basic
・大切 (taisetsu) = Important
・あいさつ (aisatsu) = Greetings

[Overall Meaning]
Accidents rokne ke efforts mein, sabse basic aur important cheez "Aisatsu" (greetings) hai.

・おはようございます (Ohayou gozaimasu) = Good morning
・おつかれさまです (Otsukaresama desu) = Thank you for your hard work (Jab koi mehnat kar raha ho)
・ご安全に (Go-anzen ni) = Stay safe (Factory mein safety wish karne ke liye)

Factory mein alag-alag kaam karne wale log jab ek doosre ko greet karte hain, toh ek team feeling banti hai aur kaam achha hota hai.

【２．１．４】 ほうれんそう (HOU-REN-SOU)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Japanese companies mein HOU-REN-SOU sabse important rule hai communication ke liye. Yeh Palak (Spinach - jise japanese mein hourensou kehte hain) ke naam par rakha gaya hai yaad rakhne ke liye.

HOU = 報告 (Houkoku) = Report
REN = 連絡 (Renraku) = Contact / Communicate
SOU = 相談 (Soudan) = Consult / Advice lena

（１）報告 (Houkoku - Report)
仕事の進捗や結果などを先輩や上司に伝えることです。
(Shigoto no shinchoku ya kekka nado wo senpai ya joushi ni tsutaeru koto desu.)
Meaning: Apne kaam ka status (shinchoku) ya result apne seniors ya boss ko batana.

（２）連絡 (Renraku - Contact/Info sharing)
仕事に関する情報や、自分のスケジュールなどを先輩や上司に伝えることです。
(Shigoto ni kansuru jouhou ya, jibun no sukejuuru nado wo senpai ya joushi ni tsutaeru koto desu.)
Meaning: Kaam se judi information ya apna schedule seniors aur boss ko batana.

（３）相談 (Soudan - Consult)
トラブルが発生した場合や、わからないことを先輩や上司に伝えることです。
(Toraburu ga hassei shita baai ya, wakaranai koto wo senpai ya joushi ni tsutaeru koto desu.)
Meaning: Jab koi problem aaye ya kuch samajh na aaye, toh senior ya boss se salah (advice) lena.
