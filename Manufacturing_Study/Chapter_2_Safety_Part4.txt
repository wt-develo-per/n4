   SSW MANUFACTURING EXAM STUDY GUIDE
   CHAPTER 2: 安全衛生 (Anzen Eisei - Safety and Hygiene) - Part 4 (FINAL)
================================================================

Is final part mein hum Risk Assessment, Absolute Safety, Safety Signs (jis par image questions aate hain) aur alag-alag kaam ki safety padhenge.

----------------------------------------------------------------
【２．４．２】 リスク・アセスメント (Risk Assessment)
----------------------------------------------------------------
仕事の中にある危険や健康を害する可能性を見つけて、その危険をなくすための方法を考える活動です。
(Shigoto no naka ni aru kiken ya kenkou wo gaisuru kanousei wo mitsukete, sono kiken wo nakusu tame no houhou wo kangaeru katsudou desu.)
Meaning: Kaam ke dauran aane wale khatron (hazards) ya sehat ko nuksan pahunchane wali cheezon ko dhoondhna aur unhe khatam karne ke tarike sochne ko Risk Assessment kehte hain.

[Risk Assessment ke 4 Steps]
① 危険を見つける (Kiken wo mitsukeru) = Khatra dhoondhna.
② 危険のレベルを考える (Kiken no reberu wo kangaeru) = Khatre ka level sochna (Kitna bada khatra hai).
③ 対策を考える (Taisaku wo kangaeru) = Bachne ka tarika (Countermeasure) sochna.
④ 対策の実行と徹底 (Taisaku no jikkou to tettei) = Us tarike ko lagu karna aur sabse follow karwana.

----------------------------------------------------------------
【２．４．３】 OSHMS (Occupational Safety and Health Management System)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Yeh company ka safety system (仕組み - shikumi) hota hai jiska goal "Safe aur Healthy workplace" banana hai. Yeh PDCA cycle ki tarah kaam karta hai:
① 計画 (Keikaku - Plan) = Khatra dekh kar Safety Goal banana aur Plan karna.
② 実行 (Jikkou - Do) = Safety rules ko follow karna aur training dena.
③ 評価 (Hyouka - Check) = Plan kaise chal raha hai, uska result (seika) check karna.
④ 改善 (Kaizen - Act) = Agar problem ho toh reason soch kar plan ko improve karna.

----------------------------------------------------------------
【２．４．４】 絶対安全 (Zettai Anzen - Absolute Safety)
----------------------------------------------------------------
絶対安全という言葉は「絶対に危なくない」という意味ですが、これはありえないと考えられています。
(Zettai anzen to iu kotoba wa "Zettai ni abunakunai" to iu imi desu ga, kore wa arienai to kangaerarete imasu.)
Meaning: "Zettai Anzen" ka matlab hai "bilkul bhi khatra nahi hona" (100% safe), lekin aesa hona namumkin (arienai) mana jata hai.
Kyunki:
- 機械は突然故障する (Kikai wa totsuzen koshou suru) = Machine achanak kharab ho sakti hai.
- 人間はうっかりミスをする (Ningen wa ukkari misu wo suru) = Insaan se galti ho sakti hai.
- 自然は予期せぬ災害を起こす (Shizen wa yoki senu saigai wo okosu) = Nature bina bataye disaster la sakti hai (jaise bhookamp).

Isliye, asali (hontou no) safety ka matlab 100% safe hona nahi, balki "Khatre ko kam se kam karna" (dekiru dake kiken wo sukunaku suru) aur "Accident hone ke chance ko kam karna" hai.


----------------------------------------------------------------
【２．５】 標識 (Hyoushiki - Signs / Symbols)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Exam mein aksar photo dekar puchte hain ki iska kya matlab hai. Inhe ache se yaad karein:

（１）禁止標識 (Kinshi hyoushiki - Prohibition Signs / Jo kaam mana hai)
Yeh signs RED circle aur cross line (⃠) wale hote hain:
- 禁煙 (Kin'en) = No Smoking
- 火気厳禁 (Kaki genkin) = Aag lagana mana hai (No Fires)
- 立入禁止 (Tachiiri kinshi) / 進入禁止 (Shinnyuu kinshi) = Andar aana mana hai (No Entry)
- 走るな (Hashiruna) = Dodna mana hai (No Running)

（２）注意表示 (Chuui hyouji - Caution Signs / Khatre se alert karne wale)
Yeh signs YELLOW triangle (△) wale hote hain:
- 一般注意 (Ippan chuui) = General Caution (!)
- 転落注意 (Tenraku chuui) = Girne ka khatra (Falling Hazard)
- 頭上注意 (Zujou chuui) = Sir ke upar dhyan dein (Watch your head)
- 感電注意 (Kanden chuui) = Bijli ka jhatka lagne ka khatra (Electric Shock)

（３）化学物質の危険有害性の表示 (Chemical Hazard Signs)
Yeh RED diamond (◇) shape mein hote hain. Chemicals ki packing par lage hote hain:
- 可燃物 (Kanenbutsu) = Aag pakadne wala (Flammable)
- 金属腐食性 (Kinzoku fushokusei) = Metal ko kharab karne wala ya skin jalane wala (Corrosive - Isme haath par acid girte hue dikhate hain)
- 健康有害性 (Kenkou yuugaisei) = Health ke liye hanikarak

（４）その他の標識 (Other Signs)
- 消火器 (Shoukaki) = Fire Extinguisher (Red sign)
- AED = Automatic defibrillator (Red sign with Heart and shock)
- 避難場所 (Hinan basho) = Emergency bachav ki jagah (Green sign with running man)


----------------------------------------------------------------
【２．６】 個別作業の安全のポイント (Points for Specific Work Safety)
----------------------------------------------------------------

（１）機械作業 (Machine Work)
- 非常停止スイッチ (Hijou teishi suicchi - Emergency Stop Switch): Khatra aane par machine ko turant rokne wala lal button. Hamesha pata hona chahiye ki ye kahan hai.

（２）感電の防止 (Electric Shock Prevention)
- 感電 (Kanden): Bijli ka jhatka. Is se dil ki dhadkan kharab ho sakti hai ya maut ho sakti hai.
- アース (Aasu - Earthing/Grounding): Machine ko ground se connect karna taki current body mein na aaye.
- 漏電 (Rouden): Current ka leak hona. Aasu is se bachata hai.

（３）運搬作業 (Lifting & Carrying Work)
- Bhaari cheez (重量物 - Juuryoubutsu) uthate waqt pehle ghutne mod kar kamar (koshi) niche karein, item ko gale lagayein (kakae), aur phir pair (ashi) sidhe karke uthayein (kamar se nahi uthana chahiye, warna dard hoga).

（４）鋳造工程 (Casting Process - Pighle hue metal ka kaam)
- Aas-paas bahut garmi (kouon) hoti hai, isliye 熱射病 (Nesshabyou - Heatstroke) ka dhyan rakhein.
- Ret (sand) aur polishing se dhool (funjin) udti hai, isliye 防塵マスク (Boujin masuku - Dust mask) pehnein.

（５）化学物質の取り扱い (Handling Chemicals)
- SDS (安全データシート - Safety Data Sheet): Chemical use karne se pehle ise zaroor padhein. Isme chemical ke khatre aur handling instructions hote hain.
- 換気装置 (Kanki souchi - Ventilation): Chemicals use karte waqt hawa paas hone ke liye ventilation chalu rakhein.
