【問題 21】(Question 21)
ドリルで穴あけ加工を行うとき、ガイドブシュを使うと加工精度が安定する。
(Doriru de anaake kakou wo okonau toki, gaido bushu wo tsukau to kakou seido ga antei suru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・穴あけ加工 (anaake kakou) = Drilling holes / Ana karna
・ガイドブシュ (gaido bushu) = Guide bush (drilling tool ko sidha rakhne ka jig)
・加工精度 (kakou seido) = Machining accuracy (Kitna sahi aur perfect ana hoga)
・安定する (antei suru) = Stable/pakka hota hai

[Overall Meaning]
Drill se hole karte waqt agar "Guide Bush" (tool ko hilne se bachane wala metal tube) ka use karein toh hole bilkul accurate (seido) banta hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: Drill karte waqt drill bit hil sakti hai, "Guide bush" usko tight pakad kar theek direction mein hole karne mein madad karta hai.

【問題 22】(Question 22)
材料の弾性係数の値が大きいほど、その材料はひずみ（変形）が出やすい。
(Zairyou no dansei keisuu no atai ga ookii hodo, sono zairyou wa hizumi (henkei) ga de yasui.)

[Word-by-Word]
・弾性係数 (dansei keisuu) = Elastic modulus / Young's modulus (Stiffness / sakhti ka map)
・ひずみ (hizumi) = Strain / Mudna / Deformation
・出やすい (de yasui) = Aasani se aata hai

[Overall Meaning]
Jis material ka "Elastic Modulus" (Dansei keisuu) jitna ZYADA hota hai, woh material utni aasani se mud (deform) jata hai.

[Answer]
B (間違っている - False)
Explanation: Elastic modulus (Young's modulus) ka matlab "Sakhti" (Stiffness) hota hai. Jis material ka Elastic modulus ZYADA hoga, wo utna hi HARD hoga aur aasani se nahi mudega (hizumi niku).

【問題 23】(Question 23)
一般構造用圧延鋼材の種類を表すＳＳ４００の４００は、引張強さを表している。
(Ippan kouzouyou atsuen kouzai no shurui wo arawasu SS400 no 400 wa, hippari tsuyosa wo arawashite iru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・一般構造用圧延鋼材 (ippan kouzouyou atsuen kouzai) = Rolled steel for general structure (Aam taur par use hone wala loha - SS)
・引張強さ (hippari tsuyosa) = Tensile strength (Khinchne par sehne ki taqat)

[Overall Meaning]
Lohae ka ek type hota hai "SS400". Isme jo '400' likha hai, uska matlab uski Tensile Strength (khinchne par taqat) 400 MPa hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: JIS standard ke hisaab se SS400 mein 400 ka matlab minimum Tensile Strength hoti hai (jise Tensile Test / Hippari shiken se napte hain).

【問題 24】(Question 24)
測定値の正確さを確認する「校正」には、決められた期間はない。
(Sokuteichi no seikakusa wo kakunin suru "kousei" ni wa, kimerareta kikan wa nai.)

[Word-by-Word]
・正確さ (seikakusa) = Accuracy / Sahi hona
・校正 (kousei) = Calibration (Check karna aur adjust karna)
・決められた期間 (kimerareta kikan) = Fixed time period / Fixed duration
・はない (wa nai) = Nahi hoti

[Overall Meaning]
Measuring tools ki accuracy check karne ke process jise "Kousei" (Calibration) kehte hain, usko karne ka koi fix time/period nahi hota (kabhi bhi kar sakte hain).

[Answer]
B (間違っている - False)
Explanation: Calibration (Kousei) ka time fix hota hai (jaise har 6 mahine ya 1 saal mein ek baar zaroor karna hota hai) taki tool hamesha accurate rahe. Ise kanoon ya company rule se "Kimerareta kikan" (fixed period) par karna zaroori hai.

【問題 25】(Question 25)
検査方法の一つに全数検査がある。
(Kensa houhou no hitotsu ni zensuu kensa ga aru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・検査方法の一つ (kensa houhou no hitotsu) = Inspection method ka ek tarika
・全数検査 (zensuu kensa) = 100% Inspection (Har ek piece check karna)

[Overall Meaning]
Product check karne ka ek tarika "100% Inspection" (Zensuu Kensa) bhi hota hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: Inspection ke do main tarike hote hain: 全数検査 (Zensuu kensa - 100% check) aur 抜取検査 (Nukitori kensa - Sampling/Thoda check).

【問題 26】(Question 26)
ノギスで、内径を測定することはできない。
(Nogisu de, naikei wo sokutei suru koto wa dekinai.)

[Word-by-Word]
・ノギス (Nogisu) = Vernier Caliper
・内径 (naikei) = Internal diameter / Andar ka size
・できない (dekinai) = Nahi kar sakte

[Overall Meaning]
Nogisu se andar ka size (hole diameter) nahi napa ja sakta.

[Answer]
B (間違っている - False)
Explanation: Nogisu se bahar ka size (Soto-gawa jaw), andar ka size (Uchi-gawa jaw se Naikei), aur gehrai (Depth bar se) teeno cheezein napi ja sakti hain.

【問題 27】(Question 27)
測定誤差は、作業者の測定スキルアップで小さくできる。
(Sokutei gosa wa, sagyousha no sokutei sukiru appu de chiisaku dekiru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・測定誤差 (sokutei gosa) = Measurement error (Napne mein galti)
・作業者の測定スキル (sagyousha no sokutei sukiru) = Worker ki napne ki skill/training
・小さくできる (chiisaku dekiru) = Kam (chota) kar sakte hain

[Overall Meaning]
Worker apni measuring skills ko theek karke (training se) napne ki galti (measurement error) ko kam kar sakta hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: Measuring error bahut se karano se hoti hai (jaise tircha dekhna - Shisa). Agar worker trained hoga toh aisi galtiyan kam hongi.

【問題 28】(Question 28)
一般的な副尺読取式のノギスの最小読取値は、0.05ｍｍである。
(Ippanteki na fukushaku yomitorishiki no nogisu no saishou yomitorichi wa, rei ten rei go miri (0.05mm) de aru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・一般的な (ippanteki na) = Normal / Aam taur par
・副尺 (fukushaku) = Vernier scale (chota wala scale)
・最小読取値 (saishou yomitorichi) = Minimum reading (sabse chota size jo nap sakte hain)

[Overall Meaning]
Normal Vernier Caliper (Nogisu) se jo sabse choti reading napi ja sakti hai wo 0.05 mm hoti hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: Aam taur par Nogisu ki accuracy 0.05mm (kabhi kabhi 0.02mm) hoti hai. Micrometer ki 0.01mm hoti hai.

【問題 29】(Question 29)
機械製図で寸法数字の前に付くＣは、面取り角度４５°を表す記号である。
(Kikai seizu de sunpou suuji no mae ni tsuku C wa, mentori kakudo yonjuugodo (45°) wo arawasu kigou de aru.)

[Word-by-Word]
・機械製図 (kikai seizu) = Mechanical drawing
・寸法数字の前 (sunpou suuji no mae) = Size wale number se pehle
・面取り角度 (mentori kakudo) = Chamfer angle (Kinara katne ka angle)

[Overall Meaning]
Drawing mein number ke aage jo 'C' likha hota hai, uska matlab 45 degree ka Chamfer (C) hota hai.

[Answer]
A (正しい - True)
Explanation: Drawing symbols mein C = Chamfer (45°), R = Radius, φ = Diameter, t = Thickness hota hai. Yeh bilkul sahi hai.

【問題 30】(Question 30)
図Ａに示す溶接記号の実形として、図Ｂは正しい。
(Zu A ni shimesu yousetsu kigou no jikkei to shite, zu B wa tadashii.)

[Word-by-Word]
・図Ａ (Zu A) = Diagram A (Jisme drawing symbol hai)
・溶接記号 (yousetsu kigou) = Welding symbol
・実形 (jikkei) = Actual shape (Real mein kaisa dikhega)
・図Ｂ (Zu B) = Diagram B

[Overall Meaning]
Diagram A mein jo welding symbol diya hai, real mein welding karne ke baad woh Diagram B jaisa dikhega. Yeh sahi hai.

* (Is question mein PDF image dekhni padti hai. Diagram A mein Base line ke NEECHE (下) V-shape bani hai aur arrow neeche ishara kar raha hai).

[Answer]
B (間違っている - False)
Explanation: Welding ka rule hai: Agar symbol Base line ke NEECHE hai, toh welding "TIR (Arrow) WALI SIDE" (samne) karni hoti hai.
Lekin Diagram B mein welding ulte (peeche) side ki hui dikhayi gayi hai (dashed line). Isliye yeh GALAT (False) hai.
