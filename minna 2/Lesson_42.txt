   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 42 (N4)
   Topic: ~ために / ~のに (Purpose & Usage)
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge:
1. ~tame ni: "...ke liye / ...karne ke maqsad se" (purpose/benefit)
2. ~no ni: "...ke liye (use hota hai)" - tool/method ke usage ke liye
3. Quantities with wa/mo: minimum aur emphasis

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~ために (~tame ni) - PURPOSE / BENEFIT
   -> "...karne ke liye" / "...ke fayede ke liye"
   -> Goal-oriented action
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (Dictionary form) + tame ni + [Action]
  Noun + NO + tame ni + [Action]

VERB + tame ni (karne ke liye):
  * Kuruma wo KAUTAME NI, chokin shite imasu.
    -> Gaadi khareedne ke liye bachat kar raha/rahi hoon.

  * Shiken ni uKARU TAME NI, mainichi benkyou shimasu.
    -> Exam pass karne ke liye roz padhta/padhti hoon.

  * Nihongo wo JOUZU NI NARU TAME NI, Nihon ni kimashita.
    -> Japanese achhi karne ke liye Japan aaya/aayi.

  * Isha NI NARU TAME NI, daigaku de benkyou shite imasu.
    -> Doctor banne ke liye university mein padh raha/rahi hoon.

NOUN + NO + tame ni (ke faide/benefit ke liye):
  * KENKOU NO TAME NI, maiasa hashirimasu.
    -> Sehat ke liye roz subah daudta/daudti hoon.

  * KAZOKU NO TAME NI, isshokenmei hataraite imasu.
    -> Parivaar ke liye bhaari mehnat karta/karti hoon.

  * SHOURAI NO TAME NI, chokin shimasu.
    -> Bhavishya ke liye savings karta/karti hoon.

  * NIHON NO TAME NI, nani ga dekimasu ka?
    -> Japan ke liye kya kar sakte hain?

TAME NI vs YOU NI:
  ~TAME NI = Dictionary form verb / noun (clear, intentional goal)
  ~YOU NI  = Potential/negative verb (ability-based goal)

  "Kuruma wo kau TAME NI chokin" = Gaadi khareedne ke liye (object goal)
  "Nihongo ga hanaseru YOU NI benkyou" = Bol sakoon isliye (ability goal)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~のに (~no ni) - USAGE / PURPOSE of a tool
   -> "Yeh cheez X karne ke liye use hoti hai"
   -> Kisi object ya method ka USE batana
   -> (Yeh Lesson 39 wala noni contrast se ALAG hai!)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (Dictionary form) + no ni + [tsukaimasu / ii desu / benri desu]

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono hasami wa hana wo KIRU NO NI tsukaimasu.
    -> Yeh kainchi phool kaatne ke liye use hoti hai.

  * Kono yakan wa mizu wo WAKASU NO NI tsukaimasu.
    -> Yeh kettle paani ubaalte ke liye use hoti hai.

  * Kono sofutsuea wa eigo wo HONYAKU SURU NO NI benri desu.
    -> Yeh software English translate karne ke liye useful hai.

  * Nani wo KIRU NO NI tsukaimasu ka?
    -> Kaatne ke liye kya use karte ho?

  * Kono chiisai hashi wa taberu NO NI muzukashii desu.
    -> Yeh chhoti chopstick se khaana mushkil hai.

COMMON PATTERNS with no ni:
  ~no ni tsukaimasu        = ...ke liye use karte hain
  ~no ni ii desu           = ...ke liye achha hai
  ~no ni benri desu        = ...ke liye suvidhaajanak hai
  ~no ni hitsuyou desu     = ...ke liye zaroori hai
  ~no ni muzukashii desu   = ...karna mushkil hai

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] Quantity + は / も
   -> Amount pe emphasis - minimum ya bahut zyada
----------------------------------------------------------------

QUANTITY + WA (minimum, at least):
  * Kono shigoto wa sukunakutomo 3-jikan WA kakarimasu.
    -> Yeh kaam kam se kam 3 ghante toh lagenge.

  * Eki made iku no ni 2-jikan WA kakaru deshou.
    -> Station jaane mein kam se kam 2 ghante toh lagenge.

  * Nihon de kekkonshiki wo suru no ni, 200-man-en WA hitsuyou desu.
    -> Japan mein shaadi karne ke liye kam se kam 20 lakh yen chahiye.

QUANTITY + MO (surprisingly large amount):
  * Eki made aruku no ni, 1-jikan MO kakarimashita.
    -> Station tak chalte 1 ghanta bhi lag gaya! (bahut zyada)

  * Kono hon wo yomu no ni, 3-jikan MO kakatta.
    -> Yeh kitaab padhne mein poore 3 ghante lag gaye!

  * Kare wa mainichi 10-jikan MO benkyou shimasu.
    -> Woh roz 10 ghante bhi padhta hai! (impressive/surprising)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 42]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* TAME NI ke saath Dictionary form (NOT te form):
  X: tabete tame ni -> GALAT
  OK: taberu tame ni -> SAHI

* TAME NI ke 2 uses yaad rakho:
  1. Verb dict + tame ni = "karne ke liye" (action goal)
  2. Noun + no tame ni = "ke liye / ke faide mein" (benefit)

* NO NI (purpose) vs NO NI (contrast - lesson 39):
  "Kiru no ni tsukaimasu" = kaatne ke liye use hoti (TOOL USE)
  "Benkyou shita noni, ochita" = padha phir bhi fail (CONTRAST)
  Context se samjho!

* Quantity + WA = minimum (at least)
  Quantity + MO = emphasis on large amount (as much as / even)
