[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein hum seekhenge ki koi kaam kar SAKTA hai ya nahi -
iske liye Potential Form use hoti hai.
Plus: dekhna/sunna naturally hona = miemasu / kikoemasu

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] POTENTIAL FORM - Verb ki nai shakti dikhana
   -> "...kar SAKTA/SAKTI hoon" wali expression
----------------------------------------------------------------

CONJUGATION RULES:

GROUP 1 (u-verbs): Last -u ko -e + ru karo
  kaku   (likhna)    -> kake-ru   -> kakemasu
  nomu   (peena)     -> nome-ru   -> nomemasu
  hanasu (bolna)     -> hanase-ru -> hanasemasu
  yomu   (padhna)    -> yome-ru   -> yomemasu
  kiku   (sunna)     -> kike-ru   -> kikemasu
  iku    (jaana)     -> ike-ru    -> ikemasu
  oyogu  (tairna)    -> oyoge-ru  -> oyogemasu
  hashiru(daudna)    -> hashire-ru-> hashiremasu

GROUP 2 (ru-verbs): -ru hatao + rareru lagao
  taberu (khaana)    -> tabe-rareru -> taberaremasu
  miru   (dekhna)    -> mi-rareru   -> miraremasu
  okiru  (uthna)     -> oki-rareru  -> okiraremasu
  neru   (sona)      -> ne-rareru   -> neraremasu

  NOTE: Spoken Japanese mein "ra" often drop ho jaata hai:
  taberareru -> tabereru (colloquial)
  miraremasu -> miremasu (colloquial)

GROUP 3 (Irregular):
  suru  (karna)  -> DEKIMASU  (can do)
  kuru  (aana)   -> koraremasu (can come)

IMPORTANT PARTICLE CHANGE!
  wo (object particle) -> ga (in potential sentences)

  X: Nihongo wo hanashimasu  -> I speak Japanese (fact)
  OK: Nihongo ga hanasemasu  -> I CAN speak Japanese (ability)

  X: Sakana wo tabemasu      -> I eat fish
  OK: Sakana ga taberaremasu -> I CAN eat fish

EXAMPLES:
  * Watashi wa Nihongo ga hanasemasu.
    -> Main Japanese bol sakta/sakti hoon.

  * Piano ga hikemasu ka?
    -> Kya aap piano baja sakte hain?

  * Kanji ga yomemasu ka?
    -> Kya kanji padh sakte ho?

  * Hayaku okiraremasu ka?
    -> Kya jaldi uth sakte ho?

  * Ashita koraremasu ka?
    -> Kya kal aa sakte ho?

NEGATIVE (can't do):
  hanasemasu -> hanasemasen (bol nahi sakta)
  taberaremasu -> taberaremasen (kha nahi sakta)

CONVERSATION:
  Q: Nihongo ga yomemasu ka?
     -> Kya Japanese padh sakte ho?

  A: Hiragana wa yomemasu ga, Kanji wa yomemasen.
     -> Hiragana padh sakta/sakti hoon, lekin Kanji nahi.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] 見えます (Miemasu) & 聞こえます (Kikoemasu)
   -> Natural sensing - apni marzi ke bina dikhna/sunna
----------------------------------------------------------------

IMPORTANT DIFFERENCE!

  MIEMASU (見えます) = Kuch naturally "dikh raha hai"
  MIRAREMASU        = Kuch "dekh sakta hoon" (deliberately)

  KIKOEMASU (聞こえます) = Kuch naturally "sun raha hai"
  KIKEMASU              = Kuch "sun sakta hoon" (deliberately)

  Particle: HAMESHA "GA" use karo

EXAMPLES:
  * Mado kara yama ga miemasu.
    -> Khidki se pahad dikh raha hai.

  * Umi ga miemasu.
    -> Samundar dikh raha hai.

  * Tooi tokoro kara koe ga kikoemasu.
    -> Door se awaaz aa rahi hai.

  * Nami no oto ga kikoemasu.
    -> Lehron ki awaaz sun rahi hai.

  * Hanabi ga miemasu yo!
    -> Aatyabaazi dikh rahi hai!

COMPARISON TABLE:
  SITUATION              | USE
  -----------------------|---------------------------
  TV dekhna chahta hoon  | terebi ga miraremasu
  Khidki se pahad dikh raha | yama ga miemasu
  Music suni ja sakti hai | ongaku ga kikemasu
  Bahar se awaaz aa rahi  | oto ga kikoemasu

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~しか ~ません (Shika ... masen)
   -> ONLY / Sirf - limitation express karna
   -> ALWAYS negative verb ke saath aata hai!
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Noun/Number] + shika + NEGATIVE verb

EXAMPLES:
  * Nihongo shika wakarimasen.
    -> Mujhe sirf Japanese samajh aati hai.

  * 500en shika arimasen.
    -> Mere paas sirf 500 yen hain.

  * Koko kara umi shika miemasen.
    -> Yahan se sirf samundar dikhta hai.

  * Hiragana shika yomemasen.
    -> Sirf Hiragana padh sakta/sakti hoon.

DIFFERENCE: dake vs shika
  * 500en dake arimasu. -> 500 yen hain (neutral)
  * 500en shika arimasen. -> Sirf 500 yen hi hain (regret/limitation feel)

  "Shika" mein ek DISAPPOINTMENT ki feeling hoti hai!

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~後で (go de) / ~後 (go)
   -> "...ke BAAD" - time duration ke baad kuch hoga
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Time Duration] + go + ni/de

EXAMPLES:
  * 5-fun go ni kite kudasai.
    -> 5 minute baad aana.

  * 1-jikan go ni dekimasu.
    -> 1 ghante baad ho jaayega.

  * Shokuji no ato de, koohii wo nomimasu.
    -> Khaana khane ke baad, coffee peeta/peeti hoon.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 27]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* Particle change yaad rakho: WO -> GA (potential sentences mein)

* miemasu/kikoemasu ke saath HAMESHA GA use karo
  "Yama GA miemasu" (NOT "wo")

* "shika" ke saath HAMESHA negative verb:
  X: 500en shika arimasu  -> GALAT
  OK: 500en shika arimasen -> SAHI

* Group 2 verbs mein colloquial ra-drop:
  taberareru = tabereru (dono correct hain, tabereru zyada common)

* "できます" ke 2 uses:
  1. Ability: Nihongo ga dekimasu = Japanese aa jaati hai
  2. Completion: Ie ga dekimashita = Ghar ban gaya
