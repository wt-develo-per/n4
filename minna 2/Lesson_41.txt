================================================================
   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 41 (N4)
   Topic: ~てあげます / ~てもらいます / ~てくれます (Actions as Favors)
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ki sirf cheezein hi nahi,
ACTIONS bhi give/receive karte hain Japanese mein!
Plus: Honorific versions bhi seekhenge.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[REVIEW: Basic Give/Receive (Lesson 7 yaad karo)]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  AGERU  = Main/koi doosre ko DETA hai (object)
  KURERU = Koi MUJHE deta hai (object)
  MORAU  = Main LETA hoon (object receive)

  Is lesson mein yehi verbs ACTIONS (te-form) ke saath use honge!

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~てあげます (~te agemasu)
   -> "Main... (doosre ke liye) karta hoon" - FAVOR KARNA
   -> I do something FOR someone (lower/equal status)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + agemasu

  Subject: MAIN / WO (hum)
  Receiver: [Person] NI

EXAMPLES:
  * Tomodachi ni Nihongo wo OSHIETE AGEMASHITA.
    -> Dost ko Japanese sikhaya (uske liye).

  * Kodomo ni hon wo YONDE AGEMASHITA.
    -> Bachche ko kitaab padh ke sunaayi.

  * Ojiisan no nimotsu wo MOTTE AGEMASHITA.
    -> Budhey ki baggage uthaayi (uske liye).

  * Imoto ni kaimono wo SHITE AGEMASHITA.
    -> Choti behen ke liye shopping ki.

CAUTION:
  "Agemasu" apne aap ke liye ya upar ke logon ke liye
  sunaai deta hai arrogant. Careful rahon!
  X: Sensei ni oshiete agemashita -> Rude lagta hai!
  OK: Tomodachi ni oshiete agemashita -> SAHI

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~てくれます (~te kuremasu)
   -> "Kisi ne MERE LIYE kiya" - DUSRE KA EHSAAN
   -> Someone does something FOR ME (their initiative)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + kuremasu

  Subject: [Person] GA/WA
  Receiver: (Watashi ni - often implied)

EXAMPLES:

  * Tomodachi ga Nihongo wo OSHIETE KUREMASHITA.
    -> Dost ne mujhe Japanese sikhaya (apni marzi se).

  * Haha ga bentou wo TSUKUTTE KUREMASHITA.
    -> Maa ne mere liye lunch banaya.

  * Sensei ga setsumei shite KUREMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne explain kiya (mere/hamre liye).

  * Yamada-san ga tetsudatte KUREMASHITA.
    -> Yamada-san ne madad ki (mere liye).

THANKS dene ke pattern:
  * Tetsudatte kurete, arigatou!
    -> Madad ke liye shukriya!

  * Oshiete kurete yokatta!
    -> Sikhaaya achha kiya!

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~てもらいます (~te moraimasu)
   -> "Maine (kisi se) karwaya / kisi ne meri madad ki"
   -> I RECEIVE the benefit of someone's action
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + moraimasu

  Beneficiary: WATASHI WA (implied)
  Helper: [Person] NI

EXAMPLES:
  * Tomodachi NI Nihongo wo OSHIETE MORAIMASHITA.
    -> Dost se Japanese sikhwaaya / dost ne sikhaya.

  * Sensei NI ronbun wo NAOSHITE MORAIMASHITA.
    -> Teacher se thesis theek karwaai.

  * Isha NI kusuri wo DASHITE MORAIMASHITA.
    -> Doctor ne dawai di (maine receive ki).

  * Imouto NI nimotsu wo MOTTE MORAIMASHITA.
    -> Choti behen se saamaan uthwaya.

REQUESTING with moraimasu:
  * Mite MORAEMASU KA? (moraeru = potential form)
    -> Dekh sakte ho? / Dekh doge?

  * Setsumei shite MORAEMASU KA?
    -> Explain kar sakte ho?

----------------------------------------------------------------
[COMPARISON TABLE: 3 Patterns]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  PATTERN         | MEANING             | SUBJECT
  ----------------|---------------------|--------------------
  te AGEMASU      | Main uske liye karta| Main/hum
  te KUREMASU     | Woh mere liye karta | Doosra insaan
  te MORAIMASU    | Main karwata/leta   | Main (benefit)

SAME SITUATION - 3 WAYS:
  Yamada-san ga nimotsu wo motte AGEMASHITA.
  -> Yamada-san ne [us insaan] ki baggage uthayi.

  Yamada-san ga nimotsu wo motte KUREMASHITA.
  -> Yamada-san ne [meri] baggage uthayi.

  [Watashi wa] Yamada-san ni nimotsu wo motte MORAIMASHITA.
  -> [Maine] Yamada-san se baggage uthwayi.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] HONORIFIC/HUMBLE FORMS
   -> Formal situations mein: Boss/Teacher/Elder ke saath
----------------------------------------------------------------

  PLAIN     | HONORIFIC/HUMBLE     | USE
  ----------|---------------------|--------------------------------
  agemasu   | sashiagemasu        | Superior ko favor karna (humble)
  agemasu   | yarimasu            | Animal/plant ko dena (lower)
  moraimasu | itadakimasu         | Superior se receive karna (humble)
  kuremasu  | kudasaimasu         | Superior meri help karta hai

EXAMPLES:
  * Sensei ni hon wo SASHIAGEMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ko kitaab di (respectful).

  * Hana ni mizu wo YARIMASHITA.
    -> Paudhon ko paani diya.

  * Shachou ni SETSUMEI SHITE ITADAKIMASHITA.
    -> President/boss ne explain kiya (humble receive).

  * Sensei ga OSHIETE KUDASAIMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne sikhaya (respectful kuremasu).

[PRACTICE SENTENCES]
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Dost ne mere liye pizza order kiya. (kureru)
   -> _______________________________________________

2. Maine dost ke liye pizza order kiya. (ageru)
   -> _______________________________________________

3. Maine dost se pizza order karwaya. (morau)
   -> _______________________________________________

4. Teacher ne explain kiya. (kudasaimasu - respectful)
   -> _______________________________________________

5. Paudhon ko paani diya. (yarimasu)
   -> _______________________________________________

ANSWERS:
1. Tomodachi ga piza wo chuumon shite kuremashita.
2. Tomodachi ni piza wo chuumon shite agemashita.
3. Tomodachi ni piza wo chuumon shite moraimashita.
4. Sensei ga setsumei shite kudasaimashita.
5. Hana ni mizu wo yarimashita.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 41]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* TEEN patterns ka FARK clear hona chahiye:
  AGEMASU  = Main karta hoon (for others)
  KUREMASU = Koi mere liye karta hai
  MORAIMASU = Main receive karta hoon (benefit)

* Kuremasu = ALWAYS speaker ya speaker ka group benefit lete hain
  "Kare ga tetsudatte kuremashita" = Usne MERI madad ki

* Honorific order:
  Normal: agemasu -> Humble to superior: sashiagemasu
  Normal: moraimasu -> Humble from superior: itadakimasu
  Normal: kuremasu -> Respectful from superior: kudasaimasu

* "yarimasu" = Only for plants, animals, children (very young)
  Adult ko "yarimasu" dena RUDE hai!

* Te morau = Often "kisi se karwaana" like requesting
  "Kakite moraimashita" = Likha gaya (mujhe benefit hua)
