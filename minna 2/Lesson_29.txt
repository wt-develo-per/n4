----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] TRANSITIVE vs INTRANSITIVE VERBS
   -> Tadoushi (他動詞) vs Jidoushi (自動詞)
----------------------------------------------------------------

EASY SAMAJHNE KA TARIKA:

  TRANSITIVE (Tadoushi) = KOI karta hai kuch
    -> Particle: WO (wo) use hota hai
    -> Focus: Karne wale INSAAN par
    -> Example: Watashi ga mado WO akemashita.
                -> Maine khidki kholI.

  INTRANSITIVE (Jidoushi) = CHEEZ ki state/condition
    -> Particle: GA use hota hai
    -> Focus: CHEEZ ki condition par
    -> Example: Mado GA aite imasu.
                -> Khidki khuli hui hai. (state)

PAIRS TABLE (SABSE ZAROORI!):
  -------------------------------------------------------
  TRANSITIVE (wo)  | INTRANSITIVE (ga) | MEANING
  -----------------|-------------------|----------------
  開ける (akeru)   | 開く (aku)         | Kholna / Khulna
  閉める (shimeru) | 閉まる (shimaru)   | Band karna / Band hona
  入れる (ireru)   | 入る (hairu)       | Daalna / Andar jaana
  出す (dasu)      | 出る (deru)        | Nikalana / Nikalna
  つける (tsukeru) | つく (tsuku)       | On karna / On hona
  消す (kesu)      | 消える (kieru)     | Off karna / Off hona
  壊す (kowasu)    | 壊れる (kowareru)  | Todna / Toot jaana
  汚す (yogosu)    | 汚れる (yogoreru)  | Ganda karna / Ganda hona
  落とす (otosu)   | 落ちる (ochiru)    | Girana / Girna
  止める (tomeru)  | 止まる (tomaru)    | Rokna / Rukna
  始める (hajimeru)| 始まる (hajimaru)  | Shuru karna / Shuru hona
  変える (kaeru)   | 変わる (kawaru)    | Badalna / Badal jaana
  -------------------------------------------------------

TRICK TO REMEMBER:
  "-eru" ending = TRANSITIVE (insaan karta hai)
  "-u / -aru / -eru" = ho sakta hai Intransitive

  Transitive = koi action kare (TO DO something TO something)
  Intransitive = cheez apne aap ki state mein ho

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] Intransitive Verb + ~ています = CURRENT STATE
   -> "Abhi cheez is condition mein hai"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Object] + GA + [Intransitive Verb te-form] + imasu

EXAMPLES:
  * Mado ga aite imasu.
    -> Khidki khuli hai. (state)

  * Denki ga tsuite imasu.
    -> Light jal rahi hai. (state)

  * Mado ga shimatte imasu.
    -> Khidki band hai. (state)

  * Isu ga kowarete imasu.
    -> Kursi toot gayi hai. (state)

  * Fuku ga yogorete imasu.
    -> Kapde gande ho gaye hain. (state)

  * Erebeeta ga tomatte imasu.
    -> Elevator ruka hua hai. (state)

TRANSITIVE vs INTRANSITIVE COMPARISON:
  TRANSITIVE  : Dare ka ga mado wo akemashita.
                -> Kisi ne khidki kholi.

  INTRANSITIVE: Mado ga aite imasu.
                -> Khidki khuli hui hai. (ab bhi)

  TRANSITIVE  : Denki wo keshite kudasai.
                -> Light band karo please.

  INTRANSITIVE: Denki ga kiete imasu.
                -> Light band hai (state).

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~てしまいます (~te shimaimasu)
   -> 2 uses: (1) Completion (2) Regret/Accident
----------------------------------------------------------------

USE 1 - COMPLETION (Kaam poora ho gaya / ho jayega)
  -> Positive nuance: Sab kuch khatam kar diya!

  * Shukudai wo yatte shimaimashita.
    -> Homework puri kar li! (khatam)

  * Mou tabete shimaimashita.
    -> Sab kha chuka/chuki hoon.

  * Zenbu yonde shimaimashita.
    -> Sab padh chuka/chuki hoon.

USE 2 - REGRET / ACCIDENT (Galti se hua / Afsos)
  -> Negative nuance: Oh no! Ye ho gaya...

  * Pasupoto wo nakushite shimaimashita.
    -> Passport kho gaya! (afsos)

  * Saifu wo otoshite shimaimashita.
    -> Wallet gir gayi! (galti se)

  * Tabete wa ikenai no ni, tabete shimaimashita.
    -> Khana nahi chahiye tha, phir bhi kha liya. (pachtawa)

  * Mado wo warete shimaimashita.
    -> Khidki toot gayi! (accident)

SPOKEN SHORT FORM:
  ~te shimau -> ~chau / ~jau (informal)
  tabete shimaimashita -> tabechaimashita
  itte shimaimashita -> icchaimashita

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~が ありました = Found something
   -> Cheez mili / Dhoondh li
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Kagi ga arimashita!
    -> Chabi mil gayi!

  * Ii shigoto ga arimashita.
    -> Achhi naukri mil gayi.

  * Ii resutoran ga arimashita.
    -> Achha restaurant mila.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 29]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* TRANSITIVE = WO particle / INTRANSITIVE = GA particle
  Ye rule bahut important hai JLPT mein!

* ~te imasu + intransitive = current STATE
  Mado ga aite imasu = Khidki KHULI HAI (state)
  (NOT "koi khol raha hai")

* ~te shimaimashita ke 2 meanings:
  1. Mou zenbu tabete shimaimashita = Sab kha liya! (done!)
  2. Okane wo nakushite shimaimashita = Paisa kho gaya! (oh no!)
  Context se samajhna padega kaun sa meaning hai.

* Spoken mein ~chaimashita use hota hai:
  nakushite shimaimashita = nakushichaimashita

* Pairs yaad rakho: akeru/aku, shimeru/shimaru, tsukeru/tsuku,
  kesu/kieru, kowasu/kowareru - ye sabse zyada aate hain!
