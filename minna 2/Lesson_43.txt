================================================================
   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 43 (N4)
   Topic: ~そうです (Appearance) + ~やすい / ~にくい
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge:
1. ~sou desu: Dekh ke andaaza lagana "lagta hai..."
2. ~yasui: Karna aasaan hai
3. ~nikui: Karna mushkil hai

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~そうです (~sou desu) - APPEARANCE
   -> Dekh ke ya feel karke andaaza lagana
   -> "...lagta hai" / "...dikh raha hai"
   -> NOTE: Yeh HEARSAY wala "sou desu" se ALAG hai!
----------------------------------------------------------------

FORMATION:

VERBS: masu-stem + sou desu
  furimasu  -> FURI + sou desu  -> Furi-sou desu
             -> Baarish hone wali lagti hai.
  taremasu  -> TARE + sou desu  -> Ochisou desu
             -> Girne wala lag raha hai.

i-ADJECTIVES: last "i" hatao + sou desu
  oishii    -> oishi + sou -> OISHISOU desu (tasty-looking)
  yasui     -> yasu + sou  -> YASUSOU desu (cheap-looking)
  takai     -> taka + sou  -> TAKASOU desu (expensive-looking)
  omoi      -> omo + sou   -> OMOSOU desu (heavy-looking)
  muzukashii -> muzukashi + sou -> MUZUKASHISOU desu

IRREGULAR i-adj:
  ii (good) -> YOSA + sou -> YOSASOU desu  [NOT iisou!]
  nai (not) -> NASA + sou -> NASASOU desu  [NOT naisou!]

na-ADJECTIVES: "na" hatao + sou desu
  genki (na)    -> GENKISOU desu  (energetic-looking)
  shizuka (na)  -> SHIZUKASOU desu (quiet-looking)
  kantan (na)   -> KANTANSOU desu (easy-looking)
  shiawase (na) -> SHIAWASESOU desu (happy-looking)

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono ryouri wa OISHISOU desu ne!
    -> Yeh khana bahut tasty lag raha hai!

  * Sora ga KUMORI-SOU desu. Kasa wo motte ikimasu.
    -> Asmaan mein baadal lage hain. Chhatri lekar jaunga.

  * Kono nimotsu wa OMOSOU desu ne.
    -> Yeh saamaan bahut bhaari lagta hai.

  * Kare wa GENKISOU desu ne.
    -> Woh bahut energetic lag raha hai.

  * Kono mondai wa KANTANSOU desu ga, muzukashii desu.
    -> Yeh sawaal aasaan lagta hai lekin mushkil hai.

CONNECTED USE (sou na + noun):
  * OISHISOU NA keeki desu ne!
    -> Kya tasty lagne wali cake hai!

  * TAIHEN-SOU NA shigoto desu ne.
    -> Bahut mushkil lagne wala kaam hai.

NEGATIVE SOUSOU:
  * Amari YOKU NASASOU desu.
    -> Zyada achha nahi lagta.

  * GENKI JA NASASOU desu.
    -> Theek nahi lagte.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~やすい (~yasui) - EASY TO DO
   -> "...karna aasaan hai" / "...karne mein aasaani hai"
   -> Masu-stem + yasui (new i-adj ban jaata hai!)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (masu-stem) + yasui + desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono pen wa kaki-YASUI desu.
    -> Yeh pen se likhna aasaan hai.

  * Kono kusuri wa nomi-YASUI desu.
    -> Yeh dawai peena aasaan hai.

  * Kono hon wa yomi-YASUI desu.
    -> Yeh kitaab padhna aasaan hai.

  * Yamada-san wa hanashi-YASUI hito desu.
    -> Yamada-san se baat karna aasaan hai. (friendly person)

  * Kono heya wa sumi-YASUI desu.
    -> Yeh kamra rehne ke liye acha hai.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~にくい (~nikui) - HARD/DIFFICULT TO DO
   -> "...karna mushkil hai" / "...karne mein takleef hoti hai"
   -> Masu-stem + nikui
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (masu-stem) + nikui + desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono kanji wa kaki-NIKUI desu.
    -> Yeh kanji likhna mushkil hai.

  * Kono kusuri wa nomi-NIKUI desu.
    -> Yeh dawai peena mushkil hai.

  * Kono kotoba wa hatsushon shi-NIKUI desu.
    -> Yeh shabd uchhaarna mushkil hai.

  * Kono heya wa sumi-NIKUI desu.
    -> Yeh kamra rehne ke liye theek nahi.

  * Kare wa hanashi-NIKUI hito desu.
    -> Usse baat karna mushkil hai. (unfriendly/intimidating)

YASUI vs NIKUI COMPARISON:
  kaki-YASUI = Likhna aasaan
  kaki-NIKUI = Likhna mushkil

  Yome-YASUI = Padhna aasaan
  Yomi-NIKUI = Padhna mushkil

  Sumi-YASUI = Rehna achha
  Sumi-NIKUI = Rehna mushkil

[SOUDESU - QUICK REFERENCE]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Type      | Rule              | Example
  ----------|------------------|---------------------------
  Verb      | masu-stem + sou  | furisou (rain-likely)
  i-adj     | drop i + sou     | oishisou (tasty-looking)
  na-adj    | drop na + sou    | genkisou (energetic-looking)
  ii (good) | yosa + sou       | yosasou (good-looking)
  nai       | nasa + sou       | nasasou (not seeming to have)

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 43]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* SOU DESU (appearance) FORMATION:
  ii -> yosasou (NEVER iisou - ye galat hai!)
  nai -> nasasou (NEVER naisou - ye galat hai!)
  Yeh dono exceptions JLPT mein aate hain!

* ~sou desu (appearance) vs ~sou desu (hearsay - L47):
  APPEARANCE: adj/verb stem + sou = "lagta hai" (khud dekha)
  HEARSAY: plain form + sou = "suna hai" (kisi ne kaha)

  "Ame ga furisou desu" = Baarish hone wali lagti hai (clouds dekhe)
  "Ame ga furu sou desu" = Suna hai baarish hogi (news se)

* Yasui/nikui = I-adjective ki tarah conjugate hota hai:
  kakiyasui -> kakiyasuku nai (negative)
  kakiyasui -> kakiyasukatta (past)

* "Hanashiyasui hito" = Friendly person
  "Hanashinikui hito" = Unapproachable person
  Ye daily conversation mein kaam aate hain!
