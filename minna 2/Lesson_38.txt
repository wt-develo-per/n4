   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 38 (N4)
   Topic: Nominalization with ~の (no ga / no wa / no wo)

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ki verb ko NOUN ki tarah use karna:
-> "Padhna achha hai" mein "Padhna" = noun
-> Japanese mein ~no lagaake verb ko noun banate hain

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] NOMINALIZATION with ~の (~no)
   -> Verb/Clause ko noun banane ka tarika
   -> "...karna / ...ka" wali noun phrases
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (Plain form) + no + [particle]

3 MAIN USES:

USE 1: ~のは (~no wa) - Topic banano
  -> Kisi action ko topic ki tarah batana

  * Ryouri wo SURU NO WA tanoshii desu.
    -> Khaana banana mazedaar hai.

  * Nihongo wo BENKYOU SURU NO WA muzukashii desu.
    -> Japanese padhna mushkil hai.

  * Watashi ga UMARETA NO WA Kyouto desu.
    -> Jahan main paida hua woh Kyoto hai.

  * Mainichi UCHI WO SOJI SURU NO WA taihen desu.
    -> Roz ghar saaf karna mushkil hai.

USE 2: ~のが (~no ga) - Subject as verb/adj ke object
  -> Pasand/naapasand / achha/bura / jaldi/dheere describe karna

  * Watashi wa hana wo SODATERU NO GA suki desu.
    -> Mujhe phool ugaana pasand hai.

  * Kanojo wa ARUKU NO GA hayai desu.
    -> Woh tez chalti hai. (literally: uska chalna tez hai)

  * Watashi wa OYOGU NO GA nigate desu.
    -> Mujhe tairna achha nahi lagta. (weak point)

  * Kare wa kanji wo KAKU NO GA jouzu desu.
    -> Woh kanji likhne mein mahir hai.

  COMMON PATTERNS with no ga:
  [action] NO GA suki/kirai desu = pasand/naapasand
  [action] NO GA jouzu/heta desu = mahir/kamzor
  [action] NO GA hayai/osoi desu = tez/dheema
  [action] NO GA wakaru/wakannai = samajhna/na samajhna

USE 3: ~のを (~no wo) - Object of another verb
  -> Forgetting, knowing, seeing, hearing an action

  * Kagi wo KAKERU NO WO wasuremashita.
    -> Taala lagaana bhool gaya.

  * Suzuki-san ga KEKKON SURU NO WO shitte imasu ka?
    -> Kya aap jaante hain ki Suzuki-san ki shaadi ho rahi hai?

  * Okasan ga OSHIERU NO WO mimashita.
    -> Maine dekha ki maa padha rahi thi.

  * Densha ga DERU NO WO matte imasu.
    -> Train ke jaane ka intezaar kar raha hoon.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] NO WO wasuremashita - Common Pattern
   -> "...karna bhool gaya"
----------------------------------------------------------------

  * Shukudai wo SURU NO WO wasuremashita.
    -> Homework karna bhool gaya.

  * Mado wo SHIMERU NO WO wasuremashita.
    -> Khidki band karna bhool gaya.

  * Kusuri wo NOMU NO WO wasuremashita.
    -> Dawai lena bhool gaya.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] Adjective -> Noun with ~さ (~sa)
   -> i-adjective ko noun mein badlo
   -> "...pan" / "...ki matra"
----------------------------------------------------------------

  takai (mahanga/uncha) -> TAKASA (unchai/mahangai)
  nagai (lamba)         -> NAGASA (lambai)
  omoi (bhaari)         -> OMOSA (bhaarpan/wajan)
  hiroi (chauda)        -> HIROSA (chaudaai)
  fukai (gehra)         -> FUKASA (gehraai)
  atsui (garam)         -> ATSUSA (garmi)
  samui (thanda)        -> SAMUSA (thandi)
  hayai (tez)           -> HAYASA (tezi/speed)

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono yama no TAKASA wa 3776m desu.
    -> Is pahad ki unchai 3776 meter hai.

  * Kawa no FUKASA wo hakarimashita.
    -> Nadi ki gehraai naapi.

  * Kono heya no HIROSA wa dono kurai desu ka?
    -> Is kamre ki chaudaai kitni hai?

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 38]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* ~no ga suki/kirai = Most common JLPT pattern
  "Oyogu no ga suki desu" = Tairna pasand hai

* NO WA vs NO GA vs NO WO confusion:
  WA = topic (kya hai topic?)
  GA = subject of adj (kya ho raha hai us action ke saath?)
  WO = object of verb (kya bhool gaya / kya jaanta hai?)

* Adjective + sa = Noun:
  JLPT mein measurement questions mein aata hai
  "Kono kawa no fukasa" = Nadi ki gehraai

* "Nakunarimasu" = Guzar jaana (death ke liye polite word)
  "Shinimashita" se zyada sophisticated hai

* Ookina / chiisana = ookii / chiisai ka alternative (na-adj form)
  Both correct hain but ookina/chiisana sirf noun se pehle aata hai
