================================================================
   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 32 (N4)
   Topic: ~ほうがいいです / ~でしょう / ~かもしれません
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge:
1. Salah dena: ~ほうがいいです (You should...)
2. Zyada certainty: ~でしょう (It will probably...)
3. Kam certainty: ~かもしれません (It might...)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~ほうがいいです (~hou ga ii desu)
   -> Salah / Advice dena: "...karna chahiye"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (ta-form) + hou ga ii desu      [positive advice]
  Verb (nai-form) + hou ga ii desu     [negative advice]

POSITIVE ADVICE (Karna chahiye):
  * Mainichi benkyou SHITA hou ga ii desu.
    -> Roz padhna chahiye.

  * Hayaku neta hou ga ii desu.
    -> Jaldi sona chahiye.

  * Isha ni itta hou ga ii desu.
    -> Doctor ke paas jaana chahiye.

  * Motto yasai wo tabeta hou ga ii desu.
    -> Zyada sabziyan khani chahiye.

NEGATIVE ADVICE (Nahi karna chahiye):
  * Tabako wo SUWANAI hou ga ii desu.
    -> Cigarette nahi peeni chahiye.

  * Osake wo nomanai hou ga ii desu.
    -> Sharaab nahi peeni chahiye.

  * Amari okashi wo tabenai hou ga ii desu.
    -> Zyada meetha nahi khana chahiye.

  * Muri wo shinai hou ga ii desu.
    -> Apne aap par zyada pressure mat daalo.

CONVERSATION:
  Q: Atama ga itai n desu ga...
     -> Sar dard hai...

  A: Kusuri wo nonda hou ga ii desu yo.
     -> Dawai leni chahiye.

  A: Hayaku neta hou ga ii desu.
     -> Jaldi so jaana chahiye.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~でしょう (~deshou)
   -> Zyada certainty ke saath ANDAAZA lagana
   -> "Shayad... hoga" (80-90% sure)
   -> Tabun (probably) / Kitto (surely) ke saath aata hai
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Plain form + deshou
  [na-adj / Noun + deshou]

EXAMPLES:
  * Tabun ame ga furu deshou.
    -> Shayad baarish hogi.

  * Kitto kuru deshou.
    -> Zaroor aayega/aayegi.

  * Ashita wa ii tenki deshou.
    -> Kal achha mausam hoga (probably).

  * Kare wa ima isogashii deshou.
    -> Woh ab busy hoga.

  * Sore wa muzukashii deshou.
    -> Woh mushkil hoga (I think).

QUESTION FORM (~deshou ka):
  * Kono shigoto wa ii deshou ka?
    -> Kya yeh kaam achha hoga?
  
  * Naze deshou ka?
    -> Aaakhir kyun hoga?

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~かもしれません (~kamo shiremasen)
   -> Kam certainty ke saath possibility batana
   -> "Hoga bhi sakta hai / shayad" (50% chance)
   -> Plain form ke saath aata hai
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (plain) + kamo shiremasen
  i-Adj (plain) + kamo shiremasen
  na-Adj / Noun + kamo shiremasen  [na hatao, da hatao]

EXAMPLES:
  * Ame ga furu kamo shiremasen.
    -> Baarish ho bhi sakti hai.

  * Kare wa kona kamo shiremasen.
    -> Woh aa bhi na sake.

  * Sono kotae wa tadashii kamo shiremasen.
    -> Woh jawab sahi bhi ho sakta hai.

  * Kare wa byouki kamo shiremasen.
    -> Woh bimaar bhi ho sakta hai.

  * Ashita wa hima kamo shiremasen.
    -> Kal free bhi ho sakta/sakti hoon.

CERTAINTY COMPARISON:
  ----------------------------------------
  kitto + deshou    = Zaroor hoga (90%)
  tabun + deshou    = Shayad hoga (70-80%)
  kamo shiremasen   = Ho sakta hai (50%)
  ----------------------------------------

[PRACTICE SENTENCES]
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Zukaam hai to doctor ke paas jaana chahiye.
   -> _______________________________________________

2. Kal shayad baarish hogi. (tabun + deshou)
   -> _______________________________________________

3. Woh aa bhi na sake. (kamo shiremasen)
   -> _______________________________________________

4. Raat ko late nahi sona chahiye.
   -> _______________________________________________

ANSWERS:
1. Kaze wo hiita nara, isha ni itta hou ga ii desu.
2. Ashita wa tabun ame ga furu deshou.
3. Kare wa konai kamo shiremasen.
4. Yoru osoku nenai hou ga ii desu.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 32]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* hou ga ii desu ke saath:
  POSITIVE -> TA form (shita / itta / tabeta)
  NEGATIVE -> NAI form (shinai / ikanai / tabenai)

* deshou vs kamo shiremasen:
  deshou = Speaker zyada sure hai (high probability)
  kamo shiremasen = Speaker kam sure hai (maybe)

* na-adj/noun ke saath kamo shiremasen:
  X: hima DA kamo shiremasen -> GALAT
  OK: hima kamo shiremasen -> SAHI (da hatao)
  OK: byouki kamo shiremasen -> SAHI (da hatao)

* Moshikashitara = "possibly" - kamo shiremasen ke saath use hota hai
