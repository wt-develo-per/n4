   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 30 (N4)
   Topic: ~ておきます / ~ていきます / ~てきます

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ki kaise:
1. Pehle se taiyaari karna: ~ておきます
2. Karte hue jaana: ~ていきます
3. Karke aana / aate aate karna: ~てきます

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~ておきます (~te okimasu)
   -> Koi kaam PEHLE SE kar ke rakhna (preparation)
   -> "...kar ke rakhna / pehle se kar lena"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + okimasu

3 USES:

USE 1 - PREPARATION (Taiyaari ke liye pehle karna)
  * Ryokou no mae ni, kippu wo katte okimasu.
    -> Trip se pehle, ticket khareed ke rakhunga.

  * Kaigi no mae ni, shiryou wo yonde okimashita.
    -> Meeting se pehle, documents padh ke rakhe.

  * Heya wo soujishite okimasu.
    -> Kamra saaf kar ke rakhunga (mehmaan aane se pehle).

USE 2 - DEALING WITH SITUATION (Abhi ke liye karna)
  * Hako ni irete okimasu.
    -> Dabbe mein daal ke rakhta/rakhti hoon.

  * Mado wo akete okimasu.
    -> Khidki khol ke chhor deta/deti hoon.

USE 3 - LEAVE AS IT IS (State maintain karna)
  * Sono mama ni shite okimasu.
    -> Waisa hi rehne do. (chhedna mat)

  * Mado wo shimete okite kudasai.
    -> Khidki band karke rakho please.

NEGATIVE FORM:
  te okimasen / te okanai de kudasai
  * Mado wo akete okanai de kudasai.
    -> Khidki khol ke mat chhorena.

SPOKEN SHORT FORM:
  ~te okimasu -> ~toku (informal)
  katte okimasu -> kattoku
  yonde okimasu -> yondoku

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~ていきます (~te ikimasu)
   -> Koi kaam karke JAANA / Karte hue jaana
   -> Action + movement AWAY from speaker
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + ikimasu

USES:

USE 1 - Karte hue ja raha hoon (on the way going)
  * Nomimono wo katte ikimasu.
    -> Drink khareed ke jaunga. (raaste mein)

  * Kasa wo motte ikimasu.
    -> Chhatri lekar jaunga.

  * Kooto wo kite ikimasu.
    -> Coat pehen ke jaunga.

USE 2 - Something happening gradually (moving away in time)
  * Nihongo ga jouzu ni natte ikimasu.
    -> Japanese dheere dheere achhi hoti ja rahi hai.

  * Samuku natte ikimasu.
    -> Thanda hota ja raha hai.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~てきます (~te kimasu)
   -> Koi kaam karke WAAPIS AANA
   -> "Jaa ke X karta hoon aur wapis aata hoon"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + kimasu

USES:

USE 1 - Go do something and come back
  * Chotto denwa wo kakete kimasu.
    -> Thoda phone karke aata/aati hoon.

  * Koohii wo katte kimasu.
    -> Coffee khareed ke aata/aati hoon.

  * Toire ni itte kimasu.
    -> Washroom jaa ke aata/aati hoon.

USE 2 - Something coming toward you (approaching change)
  * Ame ga futte kimashita.
    -> Baarish aane lagi.

  * Samuku natte kimashita.
    -> Thanda hone laga hai.

  * Nihongo ga wakatte kimashita.
    -> Japanese samajh aane lagi.

COMPARISON TABLE:
  ----------------------------------------
  ~te okimasu = Pehle se kar ke rakhna
  ~te ikimasu = Karke jaana (door jaana)
  ~te kimasu  = Karke aana (waapis aana)
  ----------------------------------------

QUICK EXAMPLES:
  Kasa wo motte ikimasu.   -> Chhatri lekar jaunga.
  Kasa wo motte kimasu.    -> Chhatri lekar aaunga.
  Kasa wo katte okimasu.   -> Chhatri pehle se khareed ke rakhunga.

[PRACTICE SENTENCES - KHUD BANAO]
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. Trip se pehle hotel book kar ke rakhna chahiye.
   -> _______________________________________________

2. Main coffee khareed ke aata/aati hoon. (waapis aaunga)
   -> _______________________________________________

3. Umbrella lekar jaana. (ja raha hoon)
   -> _______________________________________________

4. Dheere dheere Japanese samajh aane lagi.
   -> _______________________________________________

5. Kamra pehle se saaf kar ke rakha.
   -> _______________________________________________

ANSWERS:
1. Ryokou no mae ni, hoteru wo yoyaku shite okimasu.

3. Kasa wo motte ikimasu.
4. Nihongo ga wakatte kimashita.
5. Heya wo soujishite okimashita.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 30]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* ~te okimasu ka MATLAB samajhna zaroori hai:
  -> Koi kaam future ke liye ALREADY kar lena
  -> Exam mein "preparation" wale sentences mein aata hai

* ~te ikimasu vs ~te kimasu:
  IKIMASU = Speaker se DOOR (jaana)
  KIMASU  = Speaker ki TARAF (aana)
  
  "Katte ikimasu" = main ja raha hoon, khareed ke jaaunga
  "Katte kimasu"  = main aa raha hoon, khareed ke aaunga

* Gradual change mein bhi dono use hote hain:
  ~natte ikimasu = dheere dheere ... hota ja raha hai (going away in time)
  ~natte kimashita = ... hone laga (coming toward now)

* Spoken mein ~te okimasu -> ~toku:
  katte okimasu -> kattoku
  (N4 mein ye form recognize karna padega)

* Yoshuu = Pehle padhna (before class)
  Fukushuu = Baad mein review karna (after class)
  Dono important vocab hain!
