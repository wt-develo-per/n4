================================================================
   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 36 (N4)
   Topic: ~ように / ~ようになります / ~ようにしています
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge:
1. ~youni: Goal/Purpose ke liye karna
2. ~youni narimasu: State change - "ab kar sakta hoon"
3. ~youni shite imasu: Koshish karna (habit banana)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~ように (~you ni) - PURPOSE
   -> Koi goal achieve karne ke liye karna
   -> "...sake ke liye" / "...ho sake isliye"
   -> Potential ya negative verb ke saath aata hai
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (Plain/Potential) + you ni + Action verb

EXAMPLES:
  * Shinbun ga yomeru you ni, kanji wo benkyou shimasu.
    -> Newspaper padh sakoon isliye kanji padhta/padhti hoon.

  * Nihongo ga jouzu ni naru you ni, mainichi renshuu shimasu.
    -> Japanese achhi ho isliye roz practice karta/karti hoon.

  * Wasurenai you ni, memo shimasu.
    -> Bhool na jaun isliye note karta/karti hoon.

  * Okurenal you ni, hayaku okimashita.
    -> Late na ho isliye jaldi utha/uthi.

  * Mieru you ni, mae ni suwatte kudasai.
    -> Dikh sake isliye aage baitho.

RULE:
  ~you ni ke pehle wala verb:
  POTENTIAL form (hanaseru, yomeru) ya
  NEGATIVE form (wasurenai, okurenai)
  NAHI: Intentional action verb (X: taberu you ni iku -> wrong nuance)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~ようになります (~you ni narimasu)
   -> CHANGE OF STATE: "Pehle nahi kar sakta tha, ab kar sakta hoon"
   -> Natural/Gradual change
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (Plain/Potential) + you ni narimasu / narimashita

POSITIVE CHANGE:
  * Nihongo ga hanaseru you ni narimashita.
    -> Japanese bolna aane laga. (pehle nahi aata tha)

  * Oyogeru you ni narimashita.
    -> Tairna aane laga.

  * Kanji ga yomeru you ni narimashita.
    -> Kanji padhna aane laga.

  * Hitori de dekiru you ni narimashita.
    -> Akele karna aane laga.

NEGATIVE CHANGE (jo tha ab nahi):
  * Sakanaa ga taberareru you ni narimashita.
    -> Pehle machhali nahi khaata tha, ab khata hoon.

  * Hayaku okiru you ni narimashita.
    -> Jaldi uthna seekh liya.

  * Osake wo nomanai you ni narimashita.
    -> Sharaab peena chhod diya. (change)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~ようにしています (~you ni shite imasu)
   -> CONSCIOUS EFFORT - "Koshish karta hoon ki..."
   -> Intentional habit banana
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (dict form / nai form) + you ni shite imasu

POSITIVE (Karne ki koshish):
  * Mainichi undou suru you ni shite imasu.
    -> Roz kasrat karne ki koshish karta/karti hoon.

  * Hayaku neru you ni shite imasu.
    -> Jaldi sone ki koshish karta/karti hoon.

  * Nihongo de kangaeru you ni shite imasu.
    -> Japanese mein sochne ki koshish karta/karti hoon.

NEGATIVE (Na karne ki koshish):
  * Osake wo nomanai you ni shite imasu.
    -> Sharaab nahi peene ki koshish karta/karti hoon.

  * Kanji o wasurenai you ni shite imasu.
    -> Kanji nahi bhoolne ki koshish karta/karti hoon.

REQUEST FORM:
  * Jikan wo mamoru you ni shite kudasai.
    -> Waqt ka dhyan rakhne ki koshish karo.

  * Mainichi renshuu suru you ni shite kudasai.
    -> Roz practice karne ki koshish karo.

COMPARISON TABLE:
  -------------------------------------------------------
  ~you ni suru      = Ek kaam ke liye koshish karna
  ~you ni naru      = Apne aap change ho jaana
  ~you ni + verb    = Goal ke liye action karna
  -------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 36]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* ~you ni vs ~tame ni (dono purpose batate hain):
  ~you ni  = Potential/negative verb ke saath (ability goal)
  ~tame ni = Dictionary/noun ke saath (purpose/benefit)

  "Nihongo ga hanaseru YOU NI benkyou shimasu" = Can speak (ability)
  "Shiken no TAME NI benkyou shimasu" = Exam ke liye (purpose)

* ~you ni narimashita = Gradual NATURAL change
  ~you ni shimashita = INTENTIONAL change (decided)

* ~you ni shite imasu = Ongoing effort/habit
  N4 mein "what do you try to do?" wale questions mein aata hai

* Negative + you ni = "So that [bad thing] doesn't happen"
  "Wasurenai you ni memo shimasu" = Bhool nahi na jaun isliye
