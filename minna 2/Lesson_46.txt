   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 46 (N4)
   Topic: ~たところです / ~ているところです / ~るところです
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ~tokoro patterns:
1. ~ta tokoro desu: "Abhi abhi kiya" (just completed)
2. ~te iru tokoro desu: "Abhi kar raha hoon" (in the middle of)
3. ~ru tokoro desu: "Abhi karne wala hoon" (about to do)
Plus: ~ta bakari desu (kiya abhi abhi - L46 ka important comparison)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~たところです (~ta tokoro desu)
   -> "Abhi abhi kiya / Kal hi hua"
   -> Action bilkul abhi complete hua hai
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (ta-form) + tokoro desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Tattaima kaetta TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi abhi ghar aaya/aayi hoon.

  * Shukudai wo shita TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi abhi homework ki.

  * Hachiji no basu wa tattaima deta TOKORO desu.
    -> 8 baje ki bus abhi abhi chali gayi.

  * Gohan wo tabeta TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi abhi khaana khaya.

  * Kare ni denwa shita TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi abhi usse call kiya.

CONVERSATION:
  Q: Yamamoto-san wa imasu ka?
     -> Kya Yamamoto-san hain?

  A: Tattaima dekaketa TOKORO desu.
     -> Abhi abhi bahar gaye hain.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~ているところです (~te iru tokoro desu)
   -> "Bilkul abhi kar raha/rahi hoon" (in the middle)
   -> Kaam ke beech mein hai
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + iru tokoro desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Ima gohan wo tabete iru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi khaana kha raha/rahi hoon. (beech mein)

  * Ima shukudai wo shite iru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi homework kar raha/rahi hoon.

  * Ima hon wo yonde iru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi kitaab padh raha/rahi hoon.

  * Chouryou chuu desu. (= shite iru tokoro)
    -> Abhi kaam mein hoon. (busy)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~るところです (~ru tokoro desu)
   -> "Abhi karne wala/wali hoon" (about to start)
   -> Shuru karne se bilkul pehle
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (dictionary form) + tokoro desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Ima kara shokuji suru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi khaana shuru karne wala/wali hoon.

  * Ima kara dekakeru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi bahar jaane wala/wali hoon.

  * Ima kara benkyou suru TOKORO desu.
    -> Abhi padhai shuru karne wala/wali hoon.

  * Tattaima kaeru TOKORO deshita.
    -> Bilkul waapis jaane wala/wali tha/thi (koi aaya tab).

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~たばかりです (~ta bakari desu)
   -> "Haal hi mein kiya" (recently)
   -> Feeling: Abhi zyada waqt nahi guzra
   -> Tokoro se thoda zyada flexible (time-wise)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (ta-form) + bakari desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Nihon ni kita BAKARI desu.
    -> Japan abhi abhi (haal hi mein) aaya/aayi hoon.

  * Kono kaisha ni haitta BAKARI desu.
    -> Is company mein haal hi mein aaya/aayi hoon.

  * Gohan wo tabeta BAKARI desu.
    -> Haal hi mein khaana khaya.

  * Kekkon shita BAKARI desu.
    -> Abhi haal mein shaadi hui hai.

TOKORO vs BAKARI comparison:
  TA TOKORO = Ekdam abhi (just this second)
  TA BAKARI = Haal hi mein (recently - few minutes/hours/days ago)

  "Tabeta TOKORO desu" = Abhi abhi khaya (seconds ago)
  "Tabeta BAKARI desu" = Haal hi mein khaya (can be hours ago)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[3 TOKORO PATTERNS - QUICK SUMMARY]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  PATTERN          | TIMING          | EXAMPLE
  -----------------|-----------------|--------------------------------
  Ru + tokoro      | Karne wala hoon | Taberu tokoro (about to eat)
  Te iru + tokoro  | Kar raha hoon   | Tabete iru tokoro (eating now)
  Ta + tokoro      | Abhi abhi kiya  | Tabeta tokoro (just ate)

  [---shuru---] ru-tokoro [kaam shuru] te iru-tokoro [khatam] ta-tokoro [---baad---]

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 46]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* Teen patterns yaad rakho timing ke hisaab se:
  DICT + tokoro = About to (shuru hone wala)
  TE IRU + tokoro = In progress (ho raha hai)
  TA + tokoro = Just done (abhi abhi hua)

* Tattaima = "Just now" - ta tokoro ke saath common hai

* ~ta bakari vs ~ta tokoro:
  BAKARI = Personal feeling (time flexible)
  TOKORO = Very precise timing (just this moment)
  JLPT mein ye distinction important hai!

* "Tattaima deta tokoro desu" = Bus abhi abhi gayi
  Ye real conversation mein bahut use hota hai!
