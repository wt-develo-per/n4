   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 35 (N4)
   Topic: ~ば Conditional Form (Agar... to...)
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ~ba (eba) conditional form:
-> "Agar X ho to Y hoga" wali condition batana
-> Natural results / General truths / Advice

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~ば FORM CONJUGATION (~ba / ~eba)
   -> Conditional: "Agar... to..."
----------------------------------------------------------------

GROUP 1 (u-verbs): Last -u -> -eba
  kaku   -> kak-EBA    (likhe to)
  nomu   -> nom-EBA    (peeye to)
  hanasu -> hanas-EBA  (bole to)
  matsu  -> mat-EBA    (intezaar kare to)
  iku    -> ik-EBA     (jaaye to)
  yomu   -> yom-EBA    (padhe to)
  oyogu  -> oyog-EBA   (taire to)
  kaeru  -> kaer-EBA   (waapis jaaye to)

GROUP 2 (ru-verbs): -ru -> -reba
  taberu -> tabe-REBA  (khaye to)
  miru   -> mi-REBA    (dekhe to)
  neru   -> ne-REBA    (soye to)
  okiru  -> oki-REBA   (uthe to)

GROUP 3 (Irregular):
  suru   -> SUREBA     (kare to)
  kuru   -> KUREBA     (aaye to)

i-ADJECTIVES: -i -> -kereba
  yasui    -> yasu-KEREBA  (sasta ho to)
  ookii    -> ooki-KEREBA  (bada ho to)
  tanoshii -> tanoshi-KEREBA (maza aaye to)
  ii       -> YOKEREBA     (achha ho to) [irregular!]

na-ADJECTIVES / NOUNS: + nara (ba)
  hima (na)  -> hima NARA (BA)     (fursat ho to)
  byouki (na)-> byouki NARA (BA)   (bimaar ho to)
  gakusei    -> gakusei NARA (BA)  (student ho to)

NEGATIVE ~ba:
  Verb nai form -> nakereba
  ikanai -> ika-NAKEREBA (na jaaye to)
  tabenai -> tabe-NAKEREBA (na khaye to)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~ばいいですか (~ba ii desu ka)
   -> Advice/Instructions maangna: "Kya karun?"
   -> "Kya karna chahiye?" / "Kaise karun?"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Verb ba-form] + ii desu ka?

EXAMPLES:
  * Dou sureba ii desu ka?
    -> Kya karun? / Kaise karun?

  * Doko ni ikeba ii desu ka?
    -> Kahan jaun?

  * Nani wo tabereba ii desu ka?
    -> Kya khaun?

  * Donna hon wo yomeba ii desu ka?
    -> Kaisi kitaab padhuun?

  * Isha ni ikeba ii desu ka?
    -> Doctor ke paas jaun kya?

ANSWER PATTERNS:
  Q: Kaze wo hiita n desu ga, dou sureba ii desu ka?
     -> Zukaam hua hai, kya karun?

  A: Kusuri wo nonde, hayaku neba ii desu yo.
     -> Dawai lo aur jaldi so jao.

  A: Isha ni itta hou ga ii desu yo.
     -> Doctor ke paas jana chahiye.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~ば USES
----------------------------------------------------------------

USE 1 - Natural Consequence / General Truth
  * Haru ni nareba, hana ga sakimasu.
    -> Basant aaye to phool khilenge

  * Botan wo oseba, doa ga akimasu.
    -> Button dabaaye to darwaza khul jaayega.

  * Migi ni magare ba, eki ga arimasu.
    -> Daayein muro to station hai.

USE 2 - Advice / Recommendation (ii desu ke saath)
  * Motto benkyou sureba, jouzu ni narimasu yo.
    -> Zyada padho to acha ho jaayega.

  * Hayaku okire ba, daijoubu desu.
    -> Jaldi utho to theek rahega.

USE 3 - Condition for something ideal
  * Okane ga areba, ryokou shitai desu.
    -> Paisa hota to trip karta/karti.

  * Jikan ga areba, ikimasu.
    -> Agar waqt ho to jaunga.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~なら (~nara)
   -> Topic-based conditional: "Agar X ki baat ho to..."
   -> Speaker listens and RESPONDS based on what was said
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Nihon e iku nara, pasupoto ga hitsuyou desu.
    -> Agar Japan jaana hai to passport chahiye.

  * Ryokou suru nara, yoyaku shite okimasu.
    -> Agar trip karna hai to pehle se book kar lo.

  * Sore nara, daijoubu desu.
    -> Agar aisa hai to theek hai.

  * Yasuku naru nara, kaimasu.
    -> Agar sasta ho to kharidega.

BA vs NARA DIFFERENCE:
  ~ba = General condition / Natural result (agar to)
  ~nara = Topic-based (dusre ki baat sun ke response)

  "Yasukere ba kaimasu" = Agar sasta hoga to kharidega (general)
  "Yasui nara kaimasu" = (Tumne kaha sasta hai) To main kharida lunga

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 35]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* BA form conjugation yaad rakho:
  G1: -u -> -eba  (kaku -> kakeba)
  G2: -ru -> -reba (taberu -> tabereba)
  G3: suru -> sureba / kuru -> kureba
  i-adj: -i -> -kereba (yasui -> yasukereba)
  "ii" -> yokereba (IRREGULAR! yaad rakho)

* ~ba ii desu ka = Common N4 question pattern
  "Dou sureba ii desu ka?" = Ye sentence bahut important hai!

* ~nara = Topic-based, kisi ki baat ke response mein
  Ba form = Pure conditional (natural result)

* ~ba form ke baad PAST tense nahi aata (generally)
  Ba form = present/future condition ke liye

* "Sorenara" = "In that case" - conversation mein use hota hai
