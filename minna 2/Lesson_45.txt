   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 45 (N4)
   Topic: ~たら Conditional (If/When)

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ~tara conditional:
-> "Agar X hua to Y karunga" (hypothetical/future condition)
-> "X hone ke baad Y karunga" (time sequence)
-> N4 ka bahut important grammar!

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~たら (~tara) FORMATION
   -> Ta-form + RA lagao
   -> PAST TENSE se banata hai!
----------------------------------------------------------------

RULE: Ta-form + ra = tara

VERB ta-form + ra:
  tabeta    -> TABETARA    (agar kha liya to)
  itta      -> ITTARA      (agar gaya to)
  kaetta    -> KAETTARA    (agar waapis aaya to)
  kita      -> KITARA      (agar aa gaya to)
  shita     -> SHITARA     (agar kiya to)
  yonda     -> YONDARA     (agar padha to)
  nomu      -> nonda -> NONDARA
  neru      -> neta  -> NETARA

i-ADJECTIVE ta-form + ra:
  yasukatta -> YASUKATTARA  (agar sasta hua to)
  yokatta   -> YOKATTARA    (agar achha hua to)
  nakatta   -> NAKATTARA    (agar nahi tha to)

na-ADJECTIVE / NOUN + dattara:
  hima datta    -> HIMA DATTARA  (agar fursat ho to)
  ame datta     -> AME DATTARA   (agar baarish ho to)
  gakusei datta -> GAKUSEI DATTARA (agar student hota to)

NEGATIVE tara:
  ikanakatta -> IKANAKATTARA  (agar nahi gaya to)
  tabenakatta -> TABENAKATTARA (agar nahi khaya to)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~たら - USE 1: HYPOTHETICAL CONDITION (If)
   -> "Agar X hua / hoga to Y hoga"
   -> Future ya hypothetical situation
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Ame GA FUTTARA, pikunikku wa chuushi desu.
    -> Agar baarish ho gayi to picnic cancel hai.

  * Okane GA ATTARA, ryokou shimasu.
    -> Agar paisa hota to trip karta/karti.

  * Moshi chiketto GA YASUKATTARA, kaimasu.
    -> Agar ticket sasti ho to khareedta/khareedti.

  * Ashita HIMA DATTARA, eiga wo mimashou.
    -> Kal fursat ho to movie dekhen.

  * GOUKAKU SHITARA, yuushoku wo gochisou shimasu yo.
    -> Agar pass ho gaye to dinner khilaunga.

  * Kono kusuri WO NONDARA, naoru deshou.
    -> Yeh dawai le to theek ho jaoge.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~たら - USE 2: TIME SEQUENCE (When/After)
   -> "X hone ke baad Y karna"
   -> Ek kaam poora hone ke baad doosra kaam
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Uchi NI KAETTARA, sugu denwa shite kudasai.
    -> Ghar aa jaao to turant call karna.

  * Shigoto GA OWATTARA, nomi ni ikimashou.
    -> Kaam khatam ho to peene chalen.

  * Juu-hachi-sai NI NATTARA, unten dekimasu.
    -> 18 saal ke ho jaao to gaadi chala sakte ho.

  * Gohan WO TABETARA, sugu nete shimaimashita.
    -> Khaana khaya toh fauran so gaya.

  * Nihon NI ITTARA, Fuji-san ni noboritai desu.
    -> Japan gaya to Fuji pahad chadhunga.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~たら - USE 3: UNEXPECTED DISCOVERY
   -> Kuch kiya aur achanak kuch discover hua
   -> "...kiya to... tha!" (surprise)
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Uchi NI KAETTARA, tomodachi ga imashita.
    -> Ghar pahuncha to dost wahan tha! (unexpected)

  * Hako WO AKETARA, nani mo arimasen deshita.
    -> Daabba khola to kuch nahi tha! (surprise)

  * Mado WO AKETARA, kirei na keshiki ga miemashita.
    -> Khidki kholI to khoobsurat nazara dikha! (pleasant surprise)

  * Denwa SHITARA, chigau hito ga demashita.
    -> Phone kiya to koi aur uthaya. (unexpected)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 5] ~たらいいですか / ~たらどうですか
   -> Salah maangna ya dena
----------------------------------------------------------------

~TARA II DESU KA (advice maangna):
  * Dou SHITARA ii desu ka?
    -> Kya karun? / Kaise karun?

  * Doko ni ITTARA ii desu ka?
    -> Kahan jaun?

  * Nani wo TABETARA ii desu ka?
    -> Kya khaun?

~TARA DOU DESU KA (suggestion dena):
  * Isha ni ITTARA dou desu ka?
    -> Doctor ke paas kyun nahi jaate?

  * Motto yasasshiku ITTARA dou desu ka?
    -> Thoda aasaan mein kyun nahi batate?

  * Yasumetara dou desu ka?
    -> Thoda aaram karo na?

----------------------------------------------------------------
[TARA vs BA vs TO - COMPARISON]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  ~TARA: Specific single condition / Time sequence / Discovery
    "Kaettara, denwa shite" = Aa jaao to call karna

  ~BA: General condition / Natural consequence
    "Ikeba, wakaru" = Jaoge to samajh aayega

  ~TO: Automatic/Inevitable result / Instructions
    "Migi ni magaru to, eki ga aru" = Daayein muro to station hai

  SIMPLE RULE:
  Daily conversation mein -> TARA sabse zyada safe!
  Natural rule/instruction -> TO
  General advice -> BA

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 45]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* Tara = ta-form + ra: Bahut aasaan!
  Ta form yaad ho to tara form bhi aayega.

* Tara ke 3 uses clear hone chahiye:
  1. IF: "Ame ga futtara, ikimasen" = Baarish ho to nahi jaunga
  2. WHEN/AFTER: "Kaettara, shite" = Aa jaao to karo
  3. SURPRISE: "Aketara, nani mo nakatta" = Khola to kuch nahi tha

* ~tara dou desu ka = SUGGESTION (try this!)
  N4 conversation mein bahut common hai

* MOSHI + tara = More hypothetical:
  "MOSHI okane ga attara..." = Agar paisa hota (hypothetical)

* Unexpected discovery (use 3):
  Sentence ke end mein past tense verb aata hai (imashita, arimashita)
  Ye pattern recognize karna seekho!
