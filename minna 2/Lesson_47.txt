================================================================
   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 47 (N4)
   Topic: ~そうです (Hearsay) / ~ようです / ~によると / ~がします
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge:
1. ~sou desu (hearsay): Sunne mein aaya ki... (Lesson 43 wale se ALAG!)
2. ~you desu: Lagta hai... (apni reasoning se)
3. ~ni yoru to: ...ke mutaabiq / ...ke anusar
4. ~ga shimasu: Awaaz/khushbu/swaad mehsoos karna

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~そうです (~sou desu) - HEARSAY
   -> "Suna hai ki..." / "Kisi ne kaha ki..."
   -> Doosre se information mili hai
   -> Plain form ke saath aata hai (L43 wale se BILKUL ALAG!)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (plain form) + sou desu
  i-Adj (plain form) + sou desu
  na-Adj + DA + sou desu
  Noun + DA + sou desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Ashita ame ga FURU SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai kal baarish hogi.

  * Yamada-san wa byouki DA SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai Yamada-san bimaar hain.

  * Ano mise no ryouri wa OISHII SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai us restaurant ka khana tasty hai.

  * Kare wa Nihon ni IKU SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai woh Japan jaayega.

  * Kongetsu wa shiken ga ARU SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai is mahine exam hai.

PAST hearsay:
  * Yamada-san wa Nihon ni ITTA SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai Yamada-san Japan gaye.

  * Mukashi, koko ni otera ga ATTA SOU desu.
    -> Suna hai pehle yahan mandir tha.

SOURCE batana: ~ni yoru to + sou desu
  * Tenkiyohou NI YORU TO, ashita wa hareru SOU desu.
    -> Mausam ki khabar ke anusar, kal dhoop niklegi.

  * Nyuusu NI YORU TO, jishin ga atta SOU desu.
    -> Khabar ke mutaabiq, bhookamp aaya.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[HEARSAY vs APPEARANCE - SABSE IMPORTANT COMPARISON!]
----------------------------------------------------------------

  SOU DESU (APPEARANCE - L43):
  -> Adj/Verb STEM + sou
  -> Khud dekh ke andaaza
  -> "Oishi-SOU" = Tasty LAGTA HAI (I see it)

  SOU DESU (HEARSAY - L47):
  -> PLAIN FORM + sou
  -> Doosre se suna
  -> "Oishii SOU" = Tasty HAI, SUNA HAI (heard it)

  QUICK TRICK:
  "Ame ga furi-SOU" = Baarish wali lagti hai (clouds dekhe)
  "Ame ga furu-SOU" = Suna hai baarish hogi (weather forecast)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~ようです (~you desu)
   -> "Lagta hai..." - Apni reasoning/inference se
   -> Evidence dekh ke ya feel karke conclusion nikalna
   -> Sou desu se zyada subjective
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (plain form) + you desu
  i-Adj (plain form) + you desu
  na-Adj + NA + you desu
  Noun + NO + you desu

EXAMPLES:
  * Kare wa byouki NA YOU desu.
    -> (Uski chehra/behavior dekh ke) lagta hai bimaar hai.

  * Mou haru ni natta YOU desu.
    -> Lagta hai ab basant aa gayi. (warmth feel ki)

  * Dooko de aatta YOU na ki ga shimasu.
    -> Lagta hai kabhi mile hain (kuch yaad sa aa raha hai).

  * Kare wa okotte iru YOU desu.
    -> Lagta hai woh gusse mein hai. (behavior dekha)

  * Kono eki wa chiisai YOU desu ne.
    -> Lagta hai yeh station chota hai. (dekh ke)

YOUDESU with DOUMO:
  * DOUMO kare wa konai YOU desu.
    -> Kuch lagta hai woh nahi aayega. (uncertain feel)

  * DOUMO netsu ga aru YOU desu.
    -> Lagta hai bukhaar hai. (body feel kar ke)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~によると (~ni yoru to)
   -> "...ke mutaabiq / ...ke anusar"
   -> Source of information batana
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Source] + ni yoru to + [Information] + sou desu / you desu

SOURCES:
  Tenkiyohou NI YORU TO = Mausam ki khabar ke anusar
  Nyuusu NI YORU TO = Khabar ke mutaabiq
  Kare NI YORU TO = Uske mutaabiq
  Kono hon NI YORU TO = Is kitaab ke anusar
  Chousa NI YORU TO = Survey ke anusar

EXAMPLES:
  * Tenkiyohou NI YORU TO, ashita wa samui sou desu.
    -> Weather forecast ke mutaabiq kal thanda hoga.

  * Nyuusu NI YORU TO, yuumei na haiyuu ga kekkon shita sou desu.
    -> Khabar ke mutaabiq mashoor actor ki shaadi ho gayi.

  * Kare NI YORU TO, sono mise wa yasui sou desu.
    -> Uske mutaabiq woh dukaan sasti hai.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~がします (~ga shimasu)
   -> Sensation describe karna - awaaz/khushbu/swaad
   -> Naturally mehsoos hone wali cheez
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Sensation noun] + ga shimasu

SENSATION NOUNS:
  OTO ga shimasu     = Awaaz aa rahi hai
  KOE ga shimasu     = Awaaz sun raha hoon (person ki)
  NIOI ga shimasu    = Khushbu/Badbu aa rahi hai
  AJI ga shimasu     = Swaad aa raha hai

EXAMPLES:
  * Hen na OTO ga shimasu.
    -> Kuch ajeeb awaaz aa rahi hai.

  * Ii NIOI ga shimasu ne!
    -> Bahut achhi khushbu aa rahi hai!

  * Nani ka kogeta NIOI ga shimasu.
    -> Kuch jalane ki khushbu aa rahi hai.

  * Amai AJI ga shimasu.
    -> Meetha swaad aa raha hai.

  * Dare ka no KOE ga shimasu.
    -> Kisi ki awaaz aa rahi hai.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 47]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* SOU DESU - 2 bilkul alag uses yaad rakho:
  L43: STEM + sou = Appearance (khud dekha)
  L47: PLAIN FORM + sou = Hearsay (kisi ne kaha)

  "Oishi-SOU" (stem) = Tasty LAGTA HAI (I see it)
  "Oishii-SOU" (plain) = Tasty HAI, SUNA HAI (heard)

  Ye JLPT ka sabse common confusion point hai!

* YOU DESU = Personal inference/reasoning
  SOU DESU (hearsay) = Information from outside source

* ~ni yoru to = Source + sou desu pattern
  Tenkiyohou ni yoru to + ... sou desu = Standard news reporting

* ~ga shimasu = Sensation (oto/nioi/aji/koe)
  Object wo senses se PERCEIVE karna
  WO NAHI, GA use karo!
