   Topic: ~ながら / ~し、~し / Habitual Actions

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein teen cheezein seekhenge:
1. Ek saath do kaam karna: ~ながら
2. Multiple reasons dena: ~し、~し
3. Purani aadatein batana: ~ていました

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] Verb Stem + ながら (~nagara)
   -> Do kaam EK SAATH karna (same subject ke liye)
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb1 (masu-stem) + nagara + Verb2 (main action)

  NOTE: Verb2 = MAIN action hoti hai
        Verb1 = Saath saath hone wali secondary action

MASU-STEM kaise banate hain?
  kikimasu  -> kiki  (listen)
  tabemasu  -> tabe  (eat)
  mimasu    -> mi    (watch)
  hanashimasu -> hanashi (speak)
  arukimasu -> aruki (walk)

EXAMPLES:
  * Ongaku wo kikinagara shokuji shimasu.
    -> Main music sunate sunate khaana khata/khati hoon.

  * Terebi wo minagara benkyou shimasu.
    -> Main TV dekhate dekhate padhai karta/karti hoon.

  * Aruki nagara hanashimasu.
    -> Main chalte chalte baat karta/karti hoon.

  * Hatarakinagara Nihongo wo benkyou shite imasu.
    -> Main kaam karte karte Japanese seekh raha/rahi hoon.

  * Uta wo utainagara, ryouri shimasu.
    -> Main gaana gate gate khaana banata/banati hoon.

IMPORTANT RULES:
  1. DONO subject ek hi hona chahiye (Main hi dono kaam kar raha hoon)
     X: Watashi ga neru nagara, kare ga benkyou shimasu -> GALAT
     (Do alag log = nagara nahi chalega)

  2. Jo kaam ZYADA important ho, woh Verb 2 mein aata hai

NEGATIVE MEANING (without doing):
  * Nani mo tabenai nagara, hataraite imasu.
    -> Kuch bhi nahi khaate (hue) kaam kar raha hoon.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~し、~し (~shi, ~shi)
   -> Multiple REASONS ya qualities batana
   -> "Bhi... aur bhi..." wali feeling
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  [Plain form] + shi, [Plain form] + shi, [Conclusion]

FORMATION:
  Verb     : taberu shi, nomu shi
  i-Adj    : ookii shi, yasui shi
  na-Adj   : kirei da shi, shizuka da shi
  Noun     : gakusei da shi, Nihonjin da shi

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono mise wa yasui shi, oishii shi, yoku kimasu.
    -> Yeh dukaan sasti bhi hai, swadisht bhi hai, isliye aksar aata/aati hoon.

  * Kare wa se ga takai shi, hansamu da shi, ninki ga arimasu.
    -> Woh lamba bhi hai, handsome bhi hai, isliye popular hai.

  * Shigoto wa omoshiroi shi, kyuuryou mo ii shi, manzoku shite imasu.
    -> Kaam interesting bhi hai, talab bhi achhi hai, isliye santusht hoon.

  * Nihongo wa muzukashii shi, kanji mo ooi shi, taihen desu.
    -> Japanese mushkil bhi hai, kanji bhi zyada hain, bohot mushkil hai.

USING ~shi to give reasons:
  Q: Doushite sono mise ga suki desu ka?
     -> Woh dukaan kyun pasand hai?

  A: Yasui shi, shizuka da shi, ii n desu.
     -> Sasti bhi hai, shant bhi hai, isliye achhi hai.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~ていました (~te imashita)
   -> Purani AADAT batana: "Pahle... kiya karta/karti tha/thi"
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (te-form) + imashita

EXAMPLES:

  * Kodomo no toki, mainichi anime wo mite imashita.
    -> Bachpan mein, main roz anime dekhta/dekhti tha/thi.

  * Maiban hachiji ni nete imashita.
    -> Raat ko 8 baje sota/soti tha/thi (pahle).

  * Daigaku no toki, mainichi jogingu shite imashita.
    -> College ke waqt, roz jogging karta/karti tha/thi.

  * Mukashi wa tabako wo sutte imashita.
    -> Pehle cigarette peeta/peeti tha/thi. (ab nahi peeta)

COMPARISON:
  ~te imasu   = Abhi bhi karta hoon (current habit)
  ~te imashita = Pehle karta tha (past habit, ab nahi)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] それに (Sore ni) & それで (Sore de)
   -> Connecting sentences
----------------------------------------------------------------

  それに (Sore ni) = Moreover / Aur bhi / Upar se
    -> Aur ek reason ya info add karna

  それで (Sore de) = Therefore / So / Isliye
    -> Cause -> Result connect karna

EXAMPLES:
  * Kono resutoran wa yasui desu. Sorenni, oishii desu.
    -> Yeh restaurant sasta hai. Aur bhi, swadisht bhi hai.

  * Ame ga futte imashita. Sorede, gakkou wo yasumimashita.
    -> Baarish ho rahi thi. Isliye school nahi gaya/gayi.

  * Netsu ga arimashita. Sorede, hayaku nete imashita.
    -> Bukhaar tha. Isliye jaldi so gaya/gayi.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 28]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* NAGARA ke saath ek hi subject hona ZAROORI hai!
  "Watashi ga aruki nagara..." = SAHI
  "Kare ga..." aur "Watashi ga..." = GALAT (2 alag log)

* ~shi ke saath Plain Form aata hai (masu form NAHI!)
  X: tabemasu shi -> GALAT
  OK: taberu shi -> SAHI

* ~shi mein na-adj/noun ke baad "da shi" aata hai:
  kirei da shi / gakusei da shi

* ~te imashita = Past Habitual ("used to do")
  ~te imasu = Present Habitual/Ongoing ("do/am doing")

* Sore ni vs Sore de:
  Sore ni = Additional info (Moreover)
  Sore de = Result/Consequence (Therefore)
