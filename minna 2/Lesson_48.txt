   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 48 (N4)
   Topic: Causative Form ~させます (Make/Let someone do)
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge Causative form:
-> "...se karwaana" (force karna)
-> "...ko karne dena" (permission dena)
-> Boss/Parent/Teacher wale situations

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] CAUSATIVE FORM CONJUGATION (~saseru)
   -> "Karwana / Karne dena"
----------------------------------------------------------------

GROUP 1 (u-verbs): -u -> -aseru
  kaku   -> kak-A-SERU  -> kakasemasu
  nomu   -> nom-A-SERU  -> nomasemasu
  yomu   -> yom-A-SERU  -> yomasemasu
  hanasu -> hanas-A-SERU -> hanasasemasu
  matsu  -> mat-A-SERU  -> matasemasu
  iku    -> ik-A-SERU   -> ikasemasu
  oyogu  -> oyog-A-SERU -> oyogasemasu
  kaeru  -> kaer-A-SERU -> kaerasemasu

GROUP 2 (ru-verbs): -ru -> -saseru
  taberu -> tabe-SASERU -> tabesasemasu
  miru   -> mi-SASERU   -> misasemasu
  neru   -> ne-SASERU   -> nesasemasu
  okiru  -> oki-SASERU  -> okisasemasu

GROUP 3 (Irregular):
  suru   -> SASERU      -> sasemasu
  kuru   -> KOSASERU    -> kosasemasu

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] CAUSATIVE SENTENCES - 2 TYPES
----------------------------------------------------------------

TYPE 1: INTRANSITIVE VERB - Subject WO + Causative
  -> Kisi INSAAN ko kuch karne dena/karwana (no other object)

  STRUCTURE: [Causer] WA + [Person] WO + [Intransitive Causative]

  * Haha wa watashi WO uchi ni KAERASEMASHITA.
    -> Maa ne mujhe ghar bheja. (force)

  * Sensei wa gakusei WO TATASEMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne student ko khada kiya.

  * Watashi wa musuko WO IKASEMASHITA.
    -> Maine bete ko jaane diya. (permission)

  * Shachou wa watashi WO kaigi ni DEASEMASHITA.
    -> Boss ne mujhe meeting mein shamil hone diya.

TYPE 2: TRANSITIVE VERB - Subject NI + Causative
  -> Jab action ka ek aur object bhi ho (wo already used)

  STRUCTURE: [Causer] WA + [Person] NI + [Object] WO + [Transitive Causative]

  * Sensei wa gakusei NI iken WO IAWASEMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne students se apni raay kahlawayi.

  * Haha wa watashi NI yasai WO TABESASEMASHITA.
    -> Maa ne mujhe sabziyan khilayin. (force/let)

  * Shachou wa watashi NI houkoku WO KAKASEMASHITA.
    -> Boss ne mujhse report likhwayi.

  * Watashi wa musume NI piano WO HIAKASEMASHITA.
    -> Maine beti ko piano bajane diya.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] CAUSATIVE MEANING - Force vs Permission
   -> Context se samjho!
----------------------------------------------------------------

FORCE (Karwana - forced):
  * Sensei ga seitoWO souji SASEMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne students se safai karwayi. (order)

  * Oya ga kodomo WO juku NI IKASEMASHITA.
    -> Maa-baap ne bachche ko coaching bheja. (no choice)

  * Kare wa watashi WO matasemashita.
    -> Usne mujhe intezaar karwaya. (annoying)

PERMISSION (Karne dena):
  * Watashi wa musume WO jiyu ni ASOBASEMASHITA.
    -> Maine beti ko azaadi se khelne diya.

  * Sensei wa watashi WO hayaku KAERASETE KUREMASHITA.
    -> Teacher ne mujhe jaldi jaane diya.

  * Kono shigoto wa watashi NI YARASETE KUDASAI.
    -> Yeh kaam mujhe karne dijiye. (humble request)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] CAUSATIVE + te morau / te kureru / te ageru
   -> "...karne ki ijazat dena/lena" patterns
----------------------------------------------------------------

  ~sasete kudasai = Karne dijiye (humble request)
  * Mou ichido itte SASETE KUDASAI.
    -> Ek baar aur kahan(ne dijiye).

  * Watashi ni yarasete kudasai.
    -> Mujhe karne dijiye.

  ~sasete moraimasu = Karne ki permission lena
  * Hayaku KAERASETE MORAIMASU ka?
    -> Kya jaldi jaane ki ijazat mil sakti hai?

  ~sasete agemasu = Karne ki permission dena
  * Kyou wa hayaku KAERASETE AGEMASHITA.
    -> Aaj jaldi jaane diya. (boss ne employee ko)

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 48]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* Causative conjugation:
  G1: -u -> -aseru (kaku -> kakaseru)
  G2: -ru -> -saseru (taberu -> tabesaseru)
  G3: suru -> saseru / kuru -> kosaseru

* INTRANSITIVE verb + WO (person wo)
  TRANSITIVE verb + NI (person ni) + WO (object wo)
  Ye distinction JLPT mein bahut important hai!

* Force vs Permission context se samjho:
  Oya ga kodomo WO ikasemashita (school mein) = Bheja (force/let)
  Watashi wa musume WO asobasemashita = Khelne diya (permission)

* ~sasete kudasai = Humble request (karne dijiye)
  N4 business/polite conversation mein bahut common!

* Causative passive bhi hota hai (L48 ke baad ka topic):
  Saserareru = Kisi ne karwaya (suffering nuance)
  Is N4 book mein basic causative tak hai.
