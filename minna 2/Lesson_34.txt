   MINNA NO NIHONGO 2 - LESSON 34 (N4)
   Topic: ~とおりに / ~あとで / ~て / ~ないで
================================================================

[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge ki actions ko sequence mein kaise batate hain:
1. ~toori ni: Jaise kaha/dekha waisa karna
2. ~ato de: Karne ke BAAD
3. ~te / ~naide: Karte hue / Bina kiye

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] ~とおりに (~toori ni)
   -> "Jaise ... kiya/kaha/dekha, waisa karo"
   -> "Exactly as..." / "Following..."
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (ta-form) + toori ni + V2
  Verb (dict form) + toori ni + V2
  Noun + no toori ni + V2

EXAMPLES:

TA-FORM + toori ni (already happened/saw):
  * Watashi ga itta toori ni, kaite kudasai.
    -> Jaise maine kaha tha, waisa likho.

  * Mita toori ni, yarimashita.
    -> Jo dekha tha, waisa kiya.

  * Sensei ga oshieta toori ni, kakimashita.
    -> Teacher ne jo sikhaya tha waisa likha.

DICT FORM + toori ni (general rule):
  * Watashi ga iu toori ni, shite kudasai.
    -> Main jo keh raha hoon waisa karo.

  * Setsumeisho ni kaite aru toori ni, kumitatemashita.
    -> Manual mein jo likha tha waisa assemble kiya.

NOUN + no toori ni:
  * Chizu no toori ni, ikimashita.
    -> Map ke hisaab se gaya.

  * Setsumeisho no toori ni, kumitatemashita.
    -> Manual ke hisaab se joura.

  * Yotei no toori ni, susumimashita.
    -> Plan ke hisaab se aage badha.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] ~あとで (~ato de)
   -> "...karne ke BAAD" - Sequence of actions
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (ta-form) + ato de + V2
  Noun + no ato de + V2

EXAMPLES:
  * Shokuji no ato de, koohii wo nomimasu.
    -> Khaana khane ke baad, coffee peeta/peeti hoon.

  * Shigoto no ato de, nomi ni ikimasen ka?
    -> Kaam ke baad, peene chalen?

  * Tegami wo kaita ato de, okuri masu.
    -> Chitthi likhne ke baad, bheejunga.

  * Soji shita ato de, dekakemashita.
    -> Safai karne ke baad, bahar gaya.

  * Nihon ni kita ato de, Nihongo ga jouzu ni narimashita.
    -> Japan aane ke baad, Japanese achhi ho gayi.

DIFFERENCE: ato de vs te kara
  * ~te kara = Verb te form (zyada tight sequence)
  * ~ato de = ta form (sequence, thodi looseness)

  Te kara wo khane ke baad immediately:
  "Tabete kara, dekakemashita" = Kha ke turant nikal gaya

  Ato de (kuch der baad bhi ho sakta hai):
  "Tabeta ato de, dekakemashita" = Khane ke baad nikla

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] ~て + V2 (te form connecting)
   -> V1 karke V2 karna (method / manner)
   -> Kaise kiya - "...karke"
----------------------------------------------------------------

EXAMPLES:
  * Shouyu wo tsukete tabemasu.
    -> Soy sauce lagaake khata/khati hoon.

  * Te wo aratte, gohan wo tabemasu.
    -> Haath dhokar khaana khata/khati hoon. 

  * Megane wo kakete, hon wo yomimasu.
    -> Chashma lagaake kitaab padhta/padhti hoon.

  * Hasitte, kaisha ni ikimashita.
    -> Daudke company gayi.

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 4] ~ないで (~naide)
   -> "...kiye bina" / "...nahi karke" V2 karna
   -> Without doing V1, do V2
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  Verb (nai-form) + de + V2 

EXAMPLES:
  * Shouyu wo tsukenai de tabemasu.
    -> Soy sauce lagaaye bina khata/khati hoon.

  * Nani mo iwanai de, dekakemashita.
    -> Kuch bole bina chala/chali gaya/gayi.

  * Gohan wo tabenai de, benkyou shimashita.
    -> Khaana khaye bina padhai ki.

  * Megane wo kakenai de, terebi wo mimasu.
    -> Chashma lagaaye bina TV dekhta/dekhti hoon.

  * Nemuranai de, shigoto wo shimashita.
    -> Soye bina kaam kiya.

NAIDE vs NAKUTE:
  ~naide = Without doing (alag action - V2 alag hai)
  ~nakute = Because didn't do (reason/result)

  "Tabenai de iku" = Bina khaye jaana (deliberate)
  "Tabenakute, genki ga nai" = Nahi khaya isliye taakat nahi (reason)

[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 34]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* Toori ni ke saath:
  TA form = Past specific event (jo hua/dekha/kaha)
  DICT form = General/ongoing instruction
  NOUN + no = According to [noun]

* Ato de ke saath HAMESHA TA form:
  X: taberu ato de -> GALAT
  OK: tabeta ato de -> SAHI

* ~naide vs ~nakute confusion:
  ~naide = without doing (action based)
  ~nakute = because didn't (reason based)

* ~te = "karke" (method/manner describe karna)
  "Te wo aratte tabemasu" = Haath dhoke khana (manner)
