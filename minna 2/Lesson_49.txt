[LESSON KA FOCUS]
Is lesson mein seekhenge Keigo ka pehla hissa - Sonkeigo:
-> Superior/Respected person ke ACTIONS ke liye use karte hain
-> Boss, teacher, senior, customer ke kaam batane ke liye
-> N4 mein introduction level hai - advanced N3/N2 mein aur detail hoga

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 1] SONKEIGO - SPECIAL HONORIFIC VERBS
   -> Kuch verbs ke liye bilkul alag respectful words hain
   -> SABSE IMPORTANT - yaad karna zaroori!
----------------------------------------------------------------

  PLAIN FORM          | SONKEIGO (Respectful)    | MEANING
  --------------------|--------------------------|------------------
  imasu               | irasshaimasu             | Hain (to be/exist)
  ikimasu             | irasshaimasu             | Jaate hain
  kimasu              | irasshaimasu             | Aate hain
  tabemasu            | meshiagarimasu           | Khaate hain
  nomimasu            | meshiagarimasu           | Peete hain
  iimasu              | osshaimasu               | Kehte hain
  shimasu             | nasaimasu                | Karte hain
  mimasu              | goran ni narimasu        | Dekhte hain
  shitte imasu        | gozonji desu             | Jaante hain
  nemasu              | oyasumi ni narimasu      | Sote hain
  moraimasu           | ouke ni narimasu         | Lete hain

EXAMPLES:
  * Sensei wa ima doko NI IRASSHAIMASU KA?
    -> Teacher abhi kahan hain?

  * Shachou wa mou MESHIAGARI MASHITA KA?
    -> Kya President ji ne khaana kha liya?

  * Yamada-san wa sou OSSHAIMASHITA.
    -> Yamada-san ne aisa kaha.

  * Kono kanji wo GORAN NI NARIMASU KA?
    -> Kya aap is kanji ko dekhenge?

  * Suzuki-san no denwa bango wo GOZONJI DESU KA?
    -> Kya aap Suzuki-san ka phone number jaante hain?

  * Shachou wa nani wo NASAIMASU KA?
    -> President ji kya kar rahe hain?

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 2] O + Verb Stem + NI NARIMASU
   -> Regular verbs ko respectful banana ka formula
   -> Special verbs ke alaawa baki verbs ke liye
   -> NOTE: 1-syllable verbs aur Group 3 verbs mein NA use karo
----------------------------------------------------------------

STRUCTURE:
  O + [masu-stem] + ni narimasu

EXAMPLES:
  * O-YOMI NI NARIMASU KA?
    -> Kya aap padhenge?

  * O-MACHI NI NATTE IMASU.
    -> Intezaar kar rahe hain. (respectful)

  * O-KAERI NI NARIMASHITA.
    -> Waapis gaye. (respectful)

  * O-DENWA NI NARIMASU.
    -> Phone karenge. (respectful)

  * Shachou wa mou o-KAERI NI NARIMASHITA.
    -> President ji wapis ja chuke hain.

CANNOT USE with:
  1-mora verbs: "miru" (mi) -> x o-mi ni naru (use goran ni naru)
  Group 3: shimasu -> use nasaimasu (NOT o-shi ni naru)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[GRAMMAR 3] CONVERSATION PATTERNS - Sonkeigo
----------------------------------------------------------------

ASKING for teacher/boss:
  * Sensei wa irasshaimasu ka?
    -> Kya teacher ji hain?

  * Shachou wa irasshaimasu ka?
    -> Kya President ji hain?

CUSTOMER service:
  * Nani wo meshiagarimasu ka?
    -> Aap kya khaaenge/peenge? (restaurant)

  * Kochira ni o-suwari kudasai.
    -> Yahan baith jaiye.

RESPECTFUL requests:
  * O-namae wo o-kaki kudasai.
    -> Apna naam likhiye.

  * Kochira de o-machi kudasai.
    -> Yahan intezaar kariye.

  hito              | kata                   | Insaan (respectful)
  dare              | donata                 | Kaun (respectful)

  itsu              | itsu (same)            | Kab

POLITE EXPRESSIONS:
  * Shoushou o-machi kudasai.
    -> Thodi der rukiye please. (formal)

  * Irasshaimase!
    -> Aaiye! / Swaagat hai! (shop mein)

  * O-matase shimashita.
    -> Intezaar karwane ke liye maafi. (formal)

  * Yoroshiku onegai itashimasu.
    -> Aapki kripa chahiye. (formal)

  * O-tsukare sama deshita.
    -> Mehnat ki (formal - leaving office)

----------------------------------------------------------------
[N4 EXAM TIPS - LESSON 49]
----------------------------------------------------------------

* IRASSHAIMASU = imasu + ikimasu + kimasu (teen ka replacement!)
  Yeh JLPT mein trap question hai - teen meanings hain!

* MESHIAGARIMASU = tabemasu + nomimasu
  Customer/senior ke liye HAMESHA meshiagarimasu use karo

* GOZONJI = "Jaante hain" - shitte imasu ka respectful
  "Gozonji desu ka?" = Do you know? (very polite)

* O + stem + ni narimasu = General respectful formula
  Lekin special verbs ke liye special forms ZAROORI hain!

* Sonkeigo = DOOSRE ke actions ke liye (superior)
  Kenjougo (L50) = APNE actions ke liye (humble)
  Ye basic distinction yaad rakhna!
